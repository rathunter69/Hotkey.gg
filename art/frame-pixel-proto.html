HOTKEY.GG — CARD FRAMES · PIXEL DIRECTION (prototype, r452)

A decision prototype, not final art. Left of each pair = today's frame (nav.css rules copied verbatim; the canvas is driven by the real themes.js hkInitCardFx engine, so the motion is the shipped motion). Right = the pixel pass. Same motion semantics, quantized: borders become 2×3px stepped bands with a 45° stair corner and a 1px lighter rim (no blur, no anti-aliased gradient — every gradient cut to 3–4 hard stops); sweeps and rotations keep their period but run on steps(n); particles run on a 3px grid at 8fps as square sprites with 3-level alpha instead of continuous fades; outer glows become a 1px rim plus a dithered 2-band checker halo. Ids, earn gates and the notch taxonomy are untouched.
