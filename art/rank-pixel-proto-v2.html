HOTKEY.GG — RANK ART v2 · PIXEL REPLICAS vs THE CREST

v1 asked whether a new 16×16 grammar could carry the ladder. v2 tests the other question: can the crests we already have be replicated in pixel art rather than replaced? Every tier below shows three things — (A) today's generative heraldic crest from themes.js (top bucket), (B) a hand-drawn 32×32 pixel replica of that exact crest (same heater silhouette, the tier metal in hi/mid/lo bands over a dark plate with a 1px deep outline, the same charge redrawn pixel-by-pixel, and the same escalating furniture: rivets → bordure → mantling curls → wings small/mid/full/grand → bead/diamond/star finials), and (C) a 16×16 chip derived from the same drawing for nav pills and leaderboard rows — wings cut to nubs, furniture dropped, charge simplified. Metals are the literal PALS hex values from themes.js, so nothing drifts. Everything is inline <rect>-per-pixel SVG at integer scales only (32-grid at 64/96/192; 16-grid at 16/32/48) — the single hard constraint pixel art puts on the engine. Three bucket pips sit under every plate. Toggle light/dark: the 1px deep outline is what lets a sprite sit on a pale page.
