Round 2. Round 1 (r454) was rejected on look: eleven of the eighteen 32s were EPX-upscaled from a 16 (the "stretched" read) and the 16s were re-cuts of the SVG symbols. This round follows the §7a style contract in dev/ART_DIRECTION.md: every master is its own drawing — 32 for the card, 16 for the chip, no algorithmic upscaling anywhere — and every glyph is a physical object (keycaps, ledgers, a stopwatch, a scroll, a mug) seen from a 3/4 top-left view, lit from top-left, 1-px ink silhouette, three tones per hue, one or two spark pixels, sitting on a shared baseline with a 1-row cast shadow. Left of each pair is still today's generative SVG from themes.js. Sprites remain char-row arrays (o ink · d/m/l/h the family ramp · s shadow · w spark · fixed material chars for paper, greys and second hues), one <rect> per horizontal run, crispEdges, zero image requests. Toggle light/dark top-right: only --px-ink and --px-shadow move.
