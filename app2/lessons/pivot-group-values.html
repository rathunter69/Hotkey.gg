Lesson 139: Group dates and value settings

Data and Lookups · Pivot tables · medium · Paid

A pivot can put the export’s dates down the side, group them into weeks, and show each figure as a sum, a count, an average or a share of its column, all without a formula. The field list moves the fields; the pivot’s shortcut menu (Shift+F10) opens the list again and changes what the values show. Group the days into weeks, show revenue as a share of each site’s fortnight, average the washes, and count the days each site reported. The key is Shift+F10.

What you will do

  1. Shift+F10 opens the shortcut menu on the cell under the cursor. On a pivot it offers Show Field List (D), Refresh (R) and Show Values As (A). Go to the pivot on Cuts and open its field list again with Shift+F10, D.
  2. Put Date in Rows in place of Week: fifteen days down the side, one row each.
  3. Excel can group a date field into days, months or years itself (Alt, J, T, G). A period key in the export does the same job, and it matches every SUMIFS that reads the same column. Group the days into weeks: put Week back in Rows, the period key the export already carries.
  4. Show Values As changes what a figure means, not the data under it. % of Column Total divides each cell by its column’s total, so each site’s column adds to 100%. Close the list, then press Shift+F10, A, C: each week’s revenue as a % of Column Total.
  5. S in the field list cycles the value field through Sum, Count and Average. A new value field starts at No Calculation, so the share goes with the revenue. Reopen the list, put Total washes in Values and press S twice for the average washes per site-day.
  6. Count counts rows, whatever they hold: a day that came in with no washes still counts as a day. Count the days each site reported: “Site” alone in Rows, then “Date” in Values as a Count, and close the list.
  7. Go to Summary!G7, Riverside’s days reported: 14 there, 15 rows in the pivot, because one day came in blank.
  8. Does it tie? Watch the counts add to 90 in the grand total: one row for each site and day in the export.
