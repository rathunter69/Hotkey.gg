Lesson 28: Moving around the worksheet

Foundations · Moving · easy · Free

One arrow press moves one cell, which is slow on a real sheet. In this lesson you jump around the Weekly Sales Report with Ctrl, Home and End instead. This makes you much faster on sheets full of data.

What you will do

  1. Ctrl+↓ jumps to the edge of the data. Move from A3 (Monday) to A7 (Friday).
  2. Jump to C7, the last column of the Friday row.
  3. Home moves to column A of the current row. Return to A7.
  4. Ctrl+End jumps to the last used cell, even when that corner is empty, and Ctrl+Home goes back to A1. Go to C10, the bottom-right corner of the used area.
  5. Return to A1.
  6. Enter moves down one row and Tab moves right one column, even with nothing typed. Move to B2, the Sales header, with one Enter and one Tab.

Shortcuts in this lesson

  • Ctrl+↑/↓/←/→ Jump to the edge of the data
  • Home Start of the row
  • Ctrl+Home Go to A1
  • Ctrl+End Go to the last used cell
  • Tab Move right
  • Enter Move down
