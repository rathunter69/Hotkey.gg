Lesson 36: Format Cells, tab by tab

Foundations · The Ribbon and dialogs · medium · Free

Format Cells is one dialog box with everything: number formats, alignment, borders. In this lesson its categories answer to their first letter — N for Number, A for Center Across — and the Borders menu boxes the table. This is how a raw block becomes a page someone can read.

What you will do

  1. The Format Cells categories answer to their first letter: N Number, C Currency, P Percentage, A Center Across. Select the Sales figures B3:B7 and give them the Number format with Ctrl+1, N.
  2. Center Across Selection (Format Cells, A) centres a title over columns without merging cells. Select A1:C1 and centre the title across the table with Ctrl+1, A.
  3. Select the table A2:C7 and rule every cell with All Borders, Alt, H, B, A.
  4. With the table still selected, ring it with a thick outside border, Alt, H, B, T.
