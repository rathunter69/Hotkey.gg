Lesson 25: The style pass

Foundations · Format · medium · Free

A manager sent the Costs sheet in General format with an all-borders grid over the whole block, so it doesn’t read like the rest of the file, and Inputs and the totals block on Raw have grids too. Two ways to format at speed: Paste Special Formats copies a whole table’s format onto another table in one paste, and F4 repeats the last action on the next block, and the next. Copy the Report’s format onto Costs, then take three grids off with one command and two F4s. The key is F4.

What you will do

  1. Paste Special, T for formats, pastes the source block’s formatting cell for cell onto the destination: the desk number format, the $ on the first row, bold right-aligned headers, all of it, and none of the numbers. A block you’ve formatted once is the template for every block like it. Copy the Report’s figure block with its headers, C4:F9, and Paste Special Formats onto Costs!B3 to dress the whole table in one paste.
  2. Alt, H, B, N removes every border in the selection: one action, start to finish, which is what F4 will repeat. A grid is not structure; a top border on a total is. The grid on Costs: select the block A3:E8 with Ctrl+A and take every border off with Alt, H, B, N.
  3. F4 repeats your last action on whatever is selected now, so the borders you removed on Costs come off Inputs with one key. Inputs has the same grid: go there, select its block A3:C15 and press F4.
  4. Third block, third grid, and still one key, because F4 keeps repeating the same action until you do something else. And the totals block on Raw, H7:K13: select it and press F4 again.
  5. The answer column is bold; the inputs aren’t. Bold says "read this one". Back on Costs, Total ($/wk) is the column a reader looks for first: select E4:E8 and make it bold with Ctrl+B.
  6. Any action, any block: widths repeat too. Equal widths across a file’s figure columns is one of the things a reviewer notices without knowing why. Set Costs’ figure columns B:E to width 12 with Alt, H, O, W, then land in Inputs’ figure column B and press F4.
  7. Costs’ totals are live formulas, the ones you’ll audit in 1.6.6. Change a rent, watch the total. Does it tie? Change Domain’s rent in B4 on Costs to 2,500 and watch its Total in E4 answer, bold and with its thousands separator.
