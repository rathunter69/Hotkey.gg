Lesson 24: The style pass

Foundations · Format · medium · Free

undefined

What you will do

  1. On Costs, select the three cost columns B4:D8 and give them thousands separators with no decimals in one action, Ctrl+1 then N.
  2. F4 outside a formula repeats your last action, a format, a border, a width, an insert, on whatever is selected now: format once, F4 everywhere. The Total column needs the same format: select E4:E8 and press F4 once; the whole Format Cells action you just did lands on all five cells.
  3. The four headers B3:E3 sit over numbers, so right-align them with Alt H A R; Site in A3 sits over names and stays as it is.
  4. Total ($/wk) is the column a reader looks for first: select E4:E8 again and make it bold with Ctrl+B.
  5. A grid is not structure: select the whole block A3:E8 with Ctrl+A and take every border off with Alt H B N.
  6. Does it tie? Watch Domain’s lease in B4 change to 2,500 and its Total in E4 answer, bold and with its thousands separator.
