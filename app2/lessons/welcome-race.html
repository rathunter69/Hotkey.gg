Lesson 1: Welcome: three shortcuts

Foundations · Welcome · easy · Free

Excel is fast when your hands know the shortcuts and slow when they do not. Three rounds: watch the slow way play itself, then do the fast way with one shortcut, both on the clock. It is the whole idea of this site in under a minute.

What you will do

  1. The arrow keys move the active cell one cell at a time. Watch: the arrow key crawls down to the last invoice, one row per press.
  2. Ctrl+Arrow jumps to the edge of the data in one press. Your turn: jump back to the top of the list with Ctrl+↑.
  3. Shift+Arrow extends a selection one cell at a time. Watch: Shift+↓ selects the invoices one row per press, all the way down.
  4. Ctrl+Shift+Arrow selects to the edge of the data in one press. Your turn: select the amounts B2:B32 with Ctrl+Shift+↓ from B2.
  5. Watch: the arrow keys walk over to F20, one cell per press.
  6. Go To (Ctrl+G) jumps to any cell you can name: type the reference and press Enter. Your turn: go back to A1 with Ctrl+G, A1, Enter.
