Lesson 35: Hardcode hunt

Foundations · Present and audit · hard · Free

undefined

What you will do

  1. The audit pass is three looks: Go To Special for typed numbers among links, show formulas for a number in a formula row, the page for the rest. Alt, H, F, D, S, O, Enter, then Font Color. Typed numbers hide among the links: select C5:E10, run Go To Special for Constants, and color Cedar Park's emailed figures blue.
  2. F4 repeats the color. A formula that reads a typed figure is marked with it, so the reader knows the whole row is provisional. E9 multiplies the emailed washes, so the whole Cedar Park row is marked blue until the feed carries the site: move to E9 and press F4.
  3. Ctrl+` shows the literal inside the formula, a number typed where a reference belongs. Rewrite by pointing; the typed number was last week's washes and is already wrong. Mueller's average ticket G6 divides by a typed 1425, which Ctrl+` shows at a glance: rewrite it as =D6/C6 by pointing.
  4. Refill the row from its own formula, the fix from 1.6.5. The typed Thursday goes, the pattern returns. In the daily table Mueller's Thursday E18 is a number in a formula row: select B18:G18, F2 on the link in G18, Ctrl+Enter, Ctrl+` off.
  5. Hidden columns get found and get you questions. Unhide, and if it should be out of the way, group it. Prior week rev is hidden between H and J, and a buyer who finds one hidden column looks for more: select H:J and unhide the column.
  6. Spaces in a title break Find, sorting and every reference to the cell. Retype it clean; Center Across Selection does the centering. The title was centered by typing spaces in front of it: retype it in A1 without them, then center it across A1:I1 with Ctrl+1 then A.
  7. Every page states its currency once. Italic, near the top, exactly that wording. The units line is gone, so a reader can't tell which currency the figures are in: in A2 press Ctrl+I, then type USD unless stated.
  8. Borders off the block, gridlines off the sheet. A page someone reads has neither; the top border on a total is the only line it needs. A grid sits over the daily table with gridlines on: select B15:G21, take every border off with Alt, H, B, N, then Alt, W, V, G.
  9. Eight faults out, nothing else changed, every link still moving. That's a page ready to sign off. Does it tie? Change Cedar Park's washes in C9 to 300 and watch the Total in C11 answer: the page is clean and still live.
