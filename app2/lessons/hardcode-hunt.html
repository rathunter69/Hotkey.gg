Lesson 35: Hardcode hunt

Foundations · Present and audit · hard · Free

undefined

What you will do

  1. The audit pass is three looks: Go To Special for typed numbers among links, show formulas for a number in a formula row, the page for the rest. Typed numbers hide among the links: select C5:E10, run Go To Special for Constants, and color Cedar Park’s emailed figures blue.
  2. E9 multiplies the emailed kWh, so the whole Cedar Park row is marked blue until the feed carries the site: move to E9 and press F4.
  3. Mueller’s average price G6 divides by a typed 6850, which Ctrl+` shows at a glance: rewrite it as =D6/C6 by pointing.
  4. In the daily table Mueller’s Thursday E18 is a number in a formula row: select B18:G18, F2 on the link in G18, Ctrl+Enter, Ctrl+` off.
  5. Prior week rev is hidden between H and J, and a buyer who finds one hidden column looks for more: select H:J and press Ctrl+Shift+).
  6. The title was centered by typing spaces in front of it: retype it in A1 without them, then center it across A1:I1 with Ctrl+1 then A.
  7. The units line is gone, so a reader cannot tell which currency the figures are in: in A2 press Ctrl+I, then type USD unless stated.
  8. A grid sits over the daily table with gridlines on: select B15:G21, take every border off with Alt H B N, then Alt W V G.
  9. Does it tie? Watch Cedar Park’s kWh in C9 change to 3,000 and the Total in C11 answer: the page is clean and still live.
