Lesson 109: F9 on a part, show formulas, Go To Special at scale

Formulas and functions · Auditing · medium · Paid

undefined

What you will do

  1. In Edit mode Ctrl+→ jumps the insertion point a word at a time and Shift extends a selection inside the formula. F9 then calculates only what is selected: a range becomes its values in braces. Esc leaves the cell as it was; Enter would keep the values. Open Domain’s SUMIF in D66 with F2, select its criteria range Transactions!$B$5:$B$94, press F9 to read the codes, then Esc.
  2. Open it again, select the criterion B66 alone and press F9 to see the site code it tests, then Esc.
  3. Show formulas with Ctrl+` and read the block: a typed 1,240 and a text "12" stand out among the formulas.
  4. In Go To Special, Constants and Formulas each carry four boxes, Numbers (U), Text (X), Logicals (G) and Errors (E), all ticked at first. Untick three and the sweep picks only the fourth kind. Select the site rows C66:F71 and Go To Special, Constants with Numbers only: the 1,240 in D67 lights alone.
  5. Replace the 1,240 with the SUMIF its neighbours use: copy D68 and paste only its formula into D67 with Ctrl+Alt+V, F.
  6. Sweep C66:F71 again with Constants and Text only: the "12" in C69 lights, a number stored as text.
  7. Link C69 to South Lamar’s wash count the way its neighbours do, =C18.
  8. Turn show formulas off with Ctrl+` and read the block as values again.
  9. Does it tie? Watch a Mueller wash on Transactions change and the block move with it, D67 included.
