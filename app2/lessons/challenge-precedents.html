Lesson 210: Challenge: precedents spread

Valuation · Precedent transactions · hard · Paid

Five fresh deals on Precedents. Multiples, premiums, ages, include flags with reasons, and the range applied to Clearcoat’s EBITDA.

What you will do

  1. The deal multiple in J5:J9: enterprise value over LTM EBITDA.
  2. The premium in O5:O9 where the target was listed, a dash where it wasn’t.
  3. Each deal’s age in years in P5:P9, against the valuation date on Inputs.
  4. Include flags in Q5:Q9 with a reason beside each in column R: the stale deal and the big strategic chain out.
  5. The EV range in C36:E36: the included deals’ low quartile, median and high quartile times Clearcoat’s EBITDA on Comps.
