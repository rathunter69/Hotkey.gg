Lesson 37: Project: the weekly KPI report

Foundations · Project and assessment · hard · Free

undefined

What you will do

  1. Give the page its title in A1, bold and one size up, and the units line USD unless stated in A2 in italic.
  2. Tab the eight headers, Site to Margin %, into A4:H4; bold them, right-align and wrap C4:H4, then center the title across A1:H1.
  3. Copy the five site names from Inputs A9:A13 into A5:A9, then type the week label w/c 22 Sep in B5 and fill it down to B9.
  4. Point C5 and D5 at Raw’s I8 and J8 across sheets, E5 at C5 × the wholesale price on Inputs anchored, all three green.
  5. Point Domain’s gross profit F5 =D5-E5, average price G5 =D5/C5 and margin H5 =F5/D5, one after another with Tab.
  6. Row 5 is the page’s one formula row: select C5:H9 with Ctrl+Shift+End and fill it down the five sites with Ctrl+D.
  7. Select C5:F10, the block plus its blank edge: one Alt+= totals every column; point G10 =D10/C10 and H10 =F10/D10; type Total in A10.
  8. Format C5:F10 comma style with no decimals, G5:G10 currency 2, H5:H10 percent 1, then currency 0 on D5:F5 and again on D10:F10 with F4.
  9. The Total row is bold with a top border, never a grid: select the whole row 10 with Shift+Space, Ctrl+B, then Alt H B P.
  10. Head the daily block: A12 kWh sold by day bold, A13:G13 Site and Mon to Sat bold with the days right-aligned, A14:A18 the sites indented.
  11. Select A13:G18, Go To Special for Blanks, point one link at Raw’s I32 and Ctrl+Enter fills all thirty cells; comma 0, green.
  12. Head a Checks block at A20 with its three labels, then the three checks in B21:B23 reading 0, the first pointed at Raw’s J13, comma 0.
  13. Set columns B:F to width 12 and G:H to 14, then AutoFit column A to its site names A5:A18 and row 4 to its wrapped headers.
  14. Freeze panes at B5, gridlines off, then set the page to print: landscape, one page, rows 1:4 repeated, &[File] and &[Date] in the footer.
  15. Does it tie? Watch Domain’s Monday kWh on Raw change to 3,000 and the Total in C10 answer while the checks in B21:B23 stay zero.
