Lesson 76: Challenge: a three-sheet model prints as a clean pack

Formatting and presentation · Printing and page layout · medium · Paid

Three sheets, no print set-up. Orientation, fit, titles, one footer everywhere and the page centered: a pack a reader can print without thinking.

What you will do

  1. Turn the pack landscape with Alt, P, O, L.
  2. In Page Setup, Alt, P, S, P, fit the pack to 1 page wide by 2 tall.
  3. Repeat rows 1:5 at the top of every printed page with Print Titles, Alt, P, I.
  4. In Custom Footer, Alt+U, put &[File] left, Page &[Page] of &[Pages] in the center and &[Date] right.
  5. On Page Setup's Margins tab, center the page horizontally with Alt+Z.
