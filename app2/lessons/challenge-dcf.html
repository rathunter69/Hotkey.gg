Lesson 166: Challenge: a DCF from a given free-cash-flow line

Finance and Accounting · DCF · hard · Paid

Five years of free cash flow are given on DCF, with EBITDA, the periods, the WACC inputs and the tables’ edges. Build the WACC, both terminal values, the mid-year factors, enterprise and equity value, and one sensitivity grid.

What you will do

  1. Build the WACC in C60 from the block above it and name the cell WACC.
  2. The perpetuity value in C27, on the normalized cash flow in K16 at the growth on Inputs.
  3. The exit value in C28, FY31 EBITDA at the multiple on Inputs.
  4. Mid-year discount factors across F21:J21, on the periods in row 20.
  5. Enterprise value in C42 on the method the switch names, and equity value in C44 after FY26 net debt.
  6. Fill one sensitivity grid, perpetuity or exit multiple, against its edges.
