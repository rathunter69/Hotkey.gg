Lesson 44: Copy, cut and paste

Foundations · Copy, paste and fill · easy · Free

The clipboard moves work around the sheet: Ctrl+C copies, Ctrl+X moves, Ctrl+V lands it. In this lesson you copy the headers, move a note, drop a copy with plain Enter and cancel a marquee with Esc. These four keys are muscle memory within a week.

What you will do

  1. Ctrl+C copies, Ctrl+X cuts, Ctrl+V pastes at the selection; Esc drops the marquee. Copy the headers B2:C2 and paste them onto E2 with Ctrl+C and Ctrl+V.
  2. Cut the Prepared by label from A10 and paste it two rows down onto A12.
  3. After a copy, Enter pastes once and drops the marquee. Copy the title in A1 and drop one copy onto A14 with a single Enter.
  4. Copy B3 and then change your mind: cancel the marquee with Esc.
