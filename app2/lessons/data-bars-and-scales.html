Lesson 65: Data bars and scales, and when not to

Formatting and presentation · Conditional formatting · medium · Paid

undefined

What you will do

  1. Alt, H, L, D opens the Data Bars gallery: Enter takes the first, a blue bar in each cell sized to its value. Read the shape: winter low, summer high. On Monthly, put data bars on the revenue line C10:N10 and read the seasonal shape.
  2. On Monthly detail, the working sheet nobody prints, put data bars on the company revenue line C66:N66.
  3. Alt, H, L, S opens Color Scales: green to red by default, each cell shaded by where it sits in the range. Back on Monthly, put a color scale on chemicals and water, C13:N13, and read which months cost most.
  4. Alt, H, L, C, S is Clear Rules from Selected Cells: every rule that meets the selection goes. Monthly is a page in the book, so the decoration comes off. With C13:N13 still selected, take the scale off with Clear Rules from Selected Cells.
  5. Clearing C10:N10 would take the slow-month rule as well, since it sits on the same cells. Manage Rules deletes one rule and leaves the rest. Open Manage Rules, delete the data bars at the top of the list, and keep the slow-month rule on C10:N10.
  6. Does it tie? Watch Austin’s January retail revenue in C6 go up to 2,000 and January’s bar on row 66 grow.
