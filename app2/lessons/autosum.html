Lesson 65: AutoSum

Foundations · Basic formulas · easy · Free

The most common formula in the world has its own key. In this lesson Alt+= proposes =SUM over the numbers above or beside you, fills a whole edge of a block at once, and totals a row. Watch how much typing it saves.

What you will do

  1. AutoSum (Alt+=) proposes =SUM over the numbers above or to the left; Enter accepts it. On B8, press Alt+= and accept the proposal to total Sales.
  2. Select C3:C8 — through the empty total cell — and press Alt+= once.
  3. Select B12:F12 and let one Alt+= put the year’s total in F12.
