Lesson 12: Fix it in place

Foundations · Enter, edit, copy and fill · medium · Free

undefined

What you will do

  1. Ctrl+F opens Find: type part of what you want, Enter jumps to the next cell containing it, Esc closes the card. Mueller is misspelled somewhere in the 60-row feed: move to Raw and find Muller.
  2. In Edit mode, Home and End send the insertion point to either end of the entry and ← → step it one character; typing inserts there. Open B14 with F2, move the insertion point to just after Mu, and insert the e so it reads Mueller.
  3. Riverside lost its e in one row: find Riversid, Find Next past the first hit (a correct Riverside), and add the e.
  4. A figure sitting on the left is text, not a number; Backspace deletes the character before the insertion point, so the trailing space goes and 1,240 snaps right. C33 shows 1,240 on the left — text with a trailing space: find it, open it, delete the space and commit it as a number.
  5. Typing on a cell replaces its whole contents — no F2 when nothing in the old entry is worth keeping. E41 reads 81500 for one day of South Lamar revenue when kWh × price says 1075: find it and type 1075 over it.
  6. Ctrl+Z undoes the last change and Ctrl+Y puts it back, so a fix can be tried, read and kept. The associate asks what was there before: Ctrl+Z, and E41 shows 81500 again.
  7. The figure was wrong after all: Ctrl+Y puts 1075 back in E41.
  8. Does it tie? Watch C33, a Riverside kWh figure, change to 2,000 and Riverside’s kWh total in I10 answer.
