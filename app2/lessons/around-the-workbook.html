Lesson 8: Around the workbook

Foundations · Move and select · easy · Free

undefined

What you will do

  1. Warm up the tab keys: Ctrl+PgDn to Costs at the end, Ctrl+PgUp back to Report.
  2. Ctrl+G (or F5) jumps to anything you can name — its job is far-off or cross-sheet targets, never the next cell over. Management asks about the Airport Saturday: land on Raw!F61 in one jump.
  3. A sheet-qualified reference — Costs!A3:E8 — names the sheet, an exclamation mark, then the cell or range. Select the whole Costs table by address: Costs!A3:E8.
  4. Hop home to Report on the tab keys, then land straight back on Costs!B7 in one jump.
  5. Finish on the report page: Report!A1, one jump.
