Lesson 15: Find, replace, fill a timeline

Foundations · Enter, edit, copy and fill · medium · Free

undefined

What you will do

  1. Ctrl+H opens Replace: type what to find, Tab, what to put there, then Alt+A replaces every match on this sheet and reports how many cells changed. Eleven cells on the Report still read w/c 08 Sep: swap every one for w/c 15 Sep with Replace All and read the count — eleven.
  2. Replace All only touches the active sheet: move to Inputs and replace the stale week label in B3 the same way — the count reads one.
  3. One Airport row on the feed is spelled Airprot: move to Raw and find it with Ctrl+F.
  4. Nothing in Airprot is worth keeping: type Airport over B55.
  5. Fill Series (Alt, H, F, I, S) continues the pattern your first cells set across the selection: Mon runs to Sat, 15 and 16 run on to 20. Back on the Report, the daily table needs its day header: type Mon in B14, select B14:G14 and let Fill Series carry it to Sat.
  6. The dates go under the days: type the first two, 15 and 16, into B15 and C15 as a Tab run.
  7. Select B15:G15 and Fill Series carries 15 and 16 on to 20.
  8. Does it tie? Watch Airport’s Monday kWh in Raw’s C56 change to 3,000 and the Airport line of the site totals, I12, answer.
