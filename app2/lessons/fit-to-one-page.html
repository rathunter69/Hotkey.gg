Lesson 33: Fit to one page

Foundations · Present and audit · easy · Free

undefined

What you will do

  1. Ctrl+End shows the sheet's extent: if it's further than your table, something stray is out there and it will print. Stray formats on emptied cells are the usual cause: Delete leaves them behind, so clear them with Clear All (Alt, H, E, A, from 1.3.1), save, and Ctrl+Home brings you back to the top. Before you set the page, read how far it runs: jump to the last used cell, Z31, then come back to the top of the sheet.
  2. Page Layout › Orientation, Alt, P, O, then L for landscape or P for portrait, turns the printed page without opening a dialog. A page wider than it is tall prints landscape: turn the Report with Alt, P, O, L.
  3. Page Setup, Alt, P, S, P, holds every print setting on its tabbed pages; Alt+F picks Fit to, one page wide by one tall, and Enter is OK. Fit to one is the default for any page that goes to a reader. One page, however wide the columns run: open Page Setup, choose Fit to, leave the counts at 1 page wide by 1 tall, and OK it.
  4. Print Titles, Alt, P, I, opens Page Setup on its Sheet page; the rows typed as Rows to repeat at top, 1:4, print at the top of every page. On a one-page report it's insurance for the day it grows. The title, the units line and the header row must top every printed page: set Print Titles to rows 1:4 with Alt, P, I.
  5. Header/Footer is a tab in Page Setup, and the tabs answer to their first letter as Format Cells' do, so H reaches it; Alt+U opens Custom Footer with the cursor in the left of its three sections, and &[File] is the code for the file name. Every printed page traces back to its file. A printed page must say which file it came from: on Page Setup's Header/Footer tab, open Custom Footer and put &[File] in the left section.
  6. &[Date] prints the date on the day it's printed, so a stale copy on a desk says so. &[Page] of &[Pages] goes in the center on a multi-page pack (the challenge). A printed page must also say when it was printed: on the Header/Footer page, Alt+R moves to the right section; put &[Date] there and OK it.
  7. Print settings live beside the sheet, not in it. Ctrl+F2 shows what the printer will get; Esc comes back. Does it tie? Change Cedar Park's washes in C9 to 300 and watch the Total in C11 answer: the print set-up changed nothing on the sheet.
