Lesson 33: Fit to one page

Foundations · Present and audit · easy · Free

undefined

What you will do

  1. Before you set the page, read how far it runs: jump to the last used cell, Z31, then come back to the top of the sheet.
  2. Page Layout › Orientation, Alt P O then L for landscape or P for portrait, turns the printed page without opening a dialog box. A page wider than it is tall prints landscape: turn the Report with Alt P O L.
  3. Page Setup, Alt P S P, holds every print setting on its tabbed pages; F picks Fit to, one page wide by one page tall, and Enter is OK. One page, however wide the columns run: open Page Setup, choose Fit to, leave the counts at 1 page wide by 1 tall, and OK it.
  4. Print Titles, Alt P I, opens Page Setup on its Sheet page; the rows typed as Rows to repeat at top, 1:4, print at the top of every page. The title, the units line and the header row must top every printed page: set Print Titles to rows 1:4 with Alt P I.
  5. A printed page must say which file it came from: on Page Setup’s Header/Footer page (Alt+H), put &[File] in the left footer section.
  6. A printed page must also say when it was printed: on the Header/Footer page, Alt+R moves to the right section; put &[Date] there and OK it.
  7. Does it tie? Watch Cedar Park’s kWh in C9 change to 3,000 and the Total in C11 answer: the print set-up changed nothing on the sheet.
