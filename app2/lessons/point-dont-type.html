Lesson 26: Point, don’t type

Foundations · Formulas · medium · Free

undefined

What you will do

  1. A formula starts with = and recalculates when its inputs change; while it is open, each arrow key points at a cell and writes its reference for you. Domain’s gross profit is revenue less energy cost: build =D5-E5 in F5 by pointing at the two cells with the arrow keys, not typing them.
  2. Domain’s average price is revenue divided by kWh: build =D5/C5 in G5 by pointing, jumping the pointer across the row with Ctrl+Left.
  3. Domain’s margin is gross profit divided by revenue: build =F5/D5 in H5 the same way, with a Ctrl+Left jump to reach D5.
  4. Read one back before you trust it: open F5 with F2 so its inputs D5 and E5 light up in color, then leave it unchanged.
  5. Five sites still have no calculated lines: select F5:H10 with Domain’s three formulas at the top and fill them down with Ctrl+D.
  6. The fill carried Domain’s $ sign down the gross-profit column: put F6:F10, and only those, back in comma style with no decimals.
  7. Does it tie? Watch Domain’s kWh in C5 change to 3,000 and its average price in G5 answer.
