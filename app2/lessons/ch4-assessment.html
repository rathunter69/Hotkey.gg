Lesson 154: Assessment: a fresh export, twelve minutes

Data and Lookups · Project and assessment · hard · Paid

Dallas has sent its own fortnight: six sites you have not seen, the same misspelling in another code, a KPI page and a case sheet cut back the same way, and eight questions open. Build the same diligence pack on the clock with no help and the keyboard only, until every check reads 0. Pass, and the chapter is Verified; this is also the test-out. The key is Ctrl+Enter.

What you will do

  1. On Export replace the misspelt site code with the right one (Ctrl+H), so every row in B carries one of the six codes.
  2. List the six site codes in Lists N5:N10, prove them with COUNTIF in O5:O11, and add the check in C26.
  3. Fill the site block on Summary C5:E10 by INDEX and MATCH from Lists: the site, its capacity and its hours open.
  4. Fill the KPI block on Summary F5:M11: capacity, days, washes, utilization, the peak, member washes and share, with totals.
  5. Fill both cubes on Summary by SUMIFS on site and week: washes in C15:F21 and retail revenue in C25:F31, totals included.
  6. Sum the washes and the retail revenue between the window’s two dates in Summary C50:C51 with SUMIFS.
  7. Define Case, Cases, Cost_Per_Wash, Sites and Ticket with Alt M M D, then paste the list on Inputs from B19.
  8. Point the case picker in Scenarios C10 at =Cases and the site picker in Summary C34 at =Sites.
  9. Wire the switch on Scenarios: the live column G5:G8 by CHOOSE and INDEX on Case, then Case itself in C11 by MATCH on the picker.
  10. Put the pass-through driver in Scenarios C14: the ticket on Inputs unless C13 holds a figure.
  11. Build the outputs in Scenarios C17:C25 from the live column, from washes a year down to EBITDA and its margin.
  12. Build break-even in Scenarios C41:C45, Goal Seek C44 to 0 by changing C43, and note the answer in C46.
  13. Fill the checks in Summary C72:C77 and Scenarios C58:C60, every one a live difference reading 0.
  14. Answer the eight open questions on Q&A: mark each Answered in E and link its answer in F to the cell that holds it.
  15. Does it tie? Watch the first retail count on Export jump to 500, the cubes and the KPI block move, and every check hold at 0.
