Lesson 206: Challenge: three comps spread and a range applied

Valuation · Trading comps · hard · Paid

Three fresh comps arrive on Comps with their quarters and site counts. Build LTM, the EV build, calendarize the March company, take the median and quartiles, add EV per site, and apply the range to Clearcoat.

What you will do

  1. LTM EBITDA in J5:J7: each comp’s last four quarters, from its row in the quarters block.
  2. The EV build in E5:E7, H5:H7 and L5:L7: market cap, EV, and EV / EBITDA with NM where EBITDA isn’t positive.
  3. Calendarize Bayline Auto Spa: its weight in N19 from its year end in M19, and calendar 2025 in O19.
  4. The median of EV / EBITDA in L8, and the low and high quartiles in L10 and L11.
  5. EV per site in $m in W5:W7, and its median in W8.
  6. Apply the range: low, median and high in C35:E35, times Clearcoat’s FY26E EBITDA (IS!E24) in C36:E36.
