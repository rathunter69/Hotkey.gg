Lesson 18: Rows and columns that keep the totals honest

Foundations · Structure · medium · Free

undefined

What you will do

  1. Shift+Space, then Ctrl+Shift+=. The same insert as 1.2.3, but now watch the formula bar on C11: a SUM whose range straddles the insert grows to take the new row in. Insert inside the block, never below it. Cedar Park opened this week: select Airport’s row 9 and insert a blank row above it, and the Total in C11 follows to =SUM(C5:C10).
  2. Name the new site: type Cedar Park in A9, then fill the week label down from B8 into B9 with Ctrl+D.
  3. Enter Cedar Park’s week across C9:E9 with Tab: 210 washes, $2,940.00 revenue, $315.00 wash cost, and the totals in C11:E11 grow together.
  4. The Old code column is the old system’s: select column I from its header I4 and delete the whole column in one press.
  5. Margin % belongs before Prior week rev: insert a column at H and type Margin % in H4, and the header comes out bold.
  6. #REF! means the formula pointed at a cell that no longer exists. Delete a column a SUM reads and it breaks, loudly, which is the point. Never paste a number over a #REF!; find what it read. On Raw, delete the Revenue column E and read the site totals block: every Revenue SUM now says #REF! instead of a number.
  7. Read the error, then undo: Ctrl+Z brings the Revenue column back and Domain’s Revenue SUM in J8 reads a number again.
  8. Does it tie? Change Cedar Park’s washes in C9 to 300 and watch the Total in C11 answer.
