Lesson 86: Member tenure from join and cancel dates

Formulas and functions · Dates · medium · Paid

A member pays $30 a month until they cancel, so what a member is worth is the fee times tenure: the months from joining to cancelling, or to today if they’re still with us. The cancel column is blank for active members, and a blank subtracts as zero, so the formula has to choose the end date first. IF the cancel cell is blank, the as-of date, otherwise the cancel date. Then tenure in months, and the churn count for September. The key is IF.

What you will do

  1. Go to Members and select the Joined and Cancelled dates, E5:F44: forty members, and six of them have cancelled.
  2. A blank cell equals "", the empty text, so F5="" asks whether a cancel date is missing. Never type a 0 into a date column to mean none: 0 is a date, January 0, 1900, and every subtraction from it is wrong. The end date in G5 is =IF(F5="",$H$2,F5): the as-of date in H2 while the member is active, the cancel date once they leave.
  3. Tenure in H5 is =G5-E5 in days and in I5 =H5/30.4 in months, typed as a Tab run.
  4. Status in J5 reads the same blank: =IF(F5="","Active","Cancelled").
  5. The fee belongs to the plan, a lookup in Chapter 4. Until then it is one typed input, blue, and every value reads it through an anchor. Value to date in K5 is =I5*$L$2; then type the fee, 30, into L2 and color it blue as an input with Alt H F C.
  6. COUNTIFS counts on a date window in one cell; it comes in 3.3.2. Until then a 1 or 0 per row, added up, does the same job. Churn in L5 flags a cancel in September: =IF(AND(F5>=DATE(2026,9,1),F5<=DATE(2026,9,30)),1,0).
  7. Ctrl+D would copy K5’s $ format down the column. Paste Special, Formulas writes the formulas only, so every cell keeps its own format. Copy G5:L5, select G5:L44 from its last row with Ctrl+Shift+↑, and paste formulas with Ctrl+Alt+V, F, Enter.
  8. Total the value and the churn on row 45: K45 =SUM(K5:K44) and L45 =SUM(L5:L44), a Tab run; three members left in September.
  9. Does it tie? Watch M0001 cancel on 9/10/2026: its tenure stops, its status flips and September churn in L45 rises to 4.
