Lesson 122: The SUMIFS cube: site × week, filled both ways

Data and Lookups · Summaries from raw rows · medium · Paid

SUMIFS you know from Chapter 3; the cube is what it becomes at scale. Sites down the side, weeks across the top, and one formula in the corner cell that reads the site from its row and the week from its column ($B15 and C$14), filled across and down, so eighteen cells are one formula. The week key on the export is what the column criterion matches. Replace the pasted cube with the live one, then build the revenue cube. The key is SUMIFS.

What you will do

  1. On Summary, link the site block to the unique list: B5:B10 =Lists!N5 with Ctrl+Enter, in green.
  2. The cube’s sites in B15:B20 read the block above: =$B5 with Ctrl+Enter.
  3. A cube is one SUMIFS read two ways: the site from its row and the week from its column. Anchor the column of the site ($B15) and the row of the week (C$14), and the corner cell’s formula is right in every cell of the grid. In C15, the corner: =SUMIFS(Export!$E$5:$E$94,Export!$B$5:$B$94,$B15,Export!$H$5:$H$94,C$14).
  4. Select C15:E20 and fill it, Ctrl+D down and then Ctrl+R across: eighteen cells, one formula.
  5. Type the totals over the pasted ones, F15:F20 =SUM(C15:E15) and C21:F21 =SUM(F15:F20), then set C15:F21 to Automatic font.
  6. The revenue cube the same way: B25:B30 =$B5, then C25:E30 a SUMIFS on column F against C$24, and Alt+= on C25:F31.
  7. Tie both cubes to the export in the Checks block: C72 =F21-SUM(Export!$E$5:$E$94) and C73 the same for revenue.
  8. Does it tie? Watch one export row’s retail washes change, and its cube cell, the totals and the check move with it.
