Lesson 187: Challenge: eight planted faults

Finance and Accounting · Auditing a model · hard · Paid

The linked model with eight faults: two typed numbers, a pattern break, a sign, a missing add-back, a #REF!, a check that isn’t a formula and a plug on the balance sheet. Find and fix them all, and the flag reads OK.

What you will do

  1. Put the two typed figures on the IS projection back on their rows’ formulas.
  2. Find the labor formula on Schedules that breaks pattern and refill its row from FY27.
  3. On CF, add FY28 depreciation back and put the sign of the FY29 receivables change right.
  4. Fix the #REF! the error count finds on Schedules.
  5. Make the equity roll on Checks a live difference again: =ROUND(BS!C21+BS!C22+BS!C23-BS!C24,2) across C12:J12.
  6. Take the plug out of FY31 cash on the BS so it reads the cash flow’s closing cash, and the flag reads OK.
