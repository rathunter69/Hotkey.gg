Lesson 57: Hide, unhide and freeze panes

Foundations · Rows, columns and sheets · medium · Free

Some columns are working noise, and on a long sheet the headers scroll away just when you need them. In this lesson you hide and unhide the Units column with the Ctrl chords, then freeze the top row so it stays put. Both are view moves: the data underneath never changes.

What you will do

  1. Ctrl+9 hides the selected rows and Ctrl+0 the columns; Ctrl+Shift+( and Ctrl+Shift+) unhide inside the selection. Hide column C, the Units column, with Ctrl+0.
  2. Select B2:D2 across the gap and bring Units back with Ctrl+Shift+0.
  3. Freeze Panes (Alt, W, F) keeps the rows above and columns left of the seam in view. Freeze the top row with Alt, W, F, R.
  4. Unfreeze the panes again with Alt, W, F, F.
