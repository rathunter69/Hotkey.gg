Lesson 41: Built-in formats on a P&L

Formatting and presentation · Number formats · medium · Paid

undefined

What you will do

  1. Land on C7 and read 18000.4 in the formula bar, then press Ctrl+↓ to see the revenue lines end at C10.
  2. One format per line, set on the whole line at once: in Ctrl+1, Number with 0 decimal places, the separator and (1,234) is the desk number format. Select the revenue lines C7:E10 and give them the desk number format from Ctrl+1: Number, 0 decimal places, the separator and (1,234).
  3. Select the site costs C13:E20 and repeat the format with F4.
  4. Site contribution, head office and EBITDA in C22:E24 take the same format: select them and press F4.
  5. Sites and washes in C33:E34 are counts that read the same way: select both rows and press F4.
  6. Revenue per wash in C35:E35 is dollars and cents: select the row and give it two decimals with Ctrl+Shift+1.
  7. Does it tie? Watch C7 change to 18500, and total revenue in C10 and EBITDA in C24 answer in the format you set.
