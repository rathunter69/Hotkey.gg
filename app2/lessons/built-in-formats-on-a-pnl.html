Lesson 40: Built-in formats on a P&L

Formatting and presentation · Number formats · medium · Paid

undefined

What you will do

  1. Select the figure block B5:P18 in one go, from B5 with Ctrl+Shift+→ then Ctrl+Shift+↓, and give it comma style with no decimals.
  2. General is the no-format format: Ctrl+Shift+~ (or Ctrl+1, G) returns a cell to how its value was typed, so a count dressed as $28.00 reads as 28 again. The site count in B20:P20 arrived dressed as currency: select the row and return it to General with Ctrl+Shift+~.
  3. Revenue per site in B21:P21 is a dollar figure: give it currency with Ctrl+Shift+4, then Alt H 9 twice for no decimals.
  4. On Inputs, the lease per site B9:D9 is a dollar figure: jump there with Go To and make it currency, no decimals, Ctrl+1 then C.
  5. Sites at year end in B11:D11 is a count: give it thousands separators with no decimals, Ctrl+1 then N.
  6. Does it tie? Watch FY24A’s public charging in B5 change to 9,000,000 and Total revenue in B8 answer, thousands separators and all.
