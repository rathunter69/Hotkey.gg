Lesson 161: Challenge: the schedules

Finance and Accounting · Schedules · hard · Paid

A model with its schedules built and ten projected lines missing, on fresh inputs. Rebuild them across FY27 to FY31 so every line reads its drivers.

What you will do

  1. Closing sites in F9:J9: opening plus new sites plus closures.
  2. Retail revenue in F22:J22 and membership revenue in F24:J24 from the build above them.
  3. Labor and rent in F32:J33: the cost per site on Inputs, inflated each year, times average sites.
  4. Receivables and payables in F46:J47, each its base ÷ 365 × its days on Inputs.
  5. The FY27 waterfall row in F71:J71 and closing PP&E in F65:J65.
  6. Term-loan interest in F84:J84: the rate on the average while Circ is 1, on the opening while it is 0.
