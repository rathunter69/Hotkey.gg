Lesson 166: The checks sheet from day one

Finance and Accounting · Model setup and efficiencies · medium · Paid

The Checks sheet is built before the model is, with a row for every check the model will need: the balance sheet balances, cash ties to the cash flow, the debt and PP&E schedules tie to the balance sheet, revenue ties to its build, the cost build cross-foots, equity rolls, and FY26 EBITDA ties to the Chapter 2 P&L. Each is a live difference (1.7.2), and the roll-up flag (3.6.4) sits on the Cover. As each schedule is built, its check goes live. The key is =.

What you will do

  1. Best practice: wrap every check in ROUND(…,2). Floating-point arithmetic leaves 0.0000000001 where a zero belongs, and the flag would call that a fault. On Checks, the balance check in C6, =ROUND(BS!C10-BS!C25,2), filled right to J6.
  2. The cash tie in C7, =ROUND(BS!C6-CF!C30,2), filled right to J7.
  3. A check whose schedule doesn’t exist yet stays empty with a note beside it, never a typed 0: a typed zero is a dead check that always passes, and the roll-up reads an empty cell as nothing. Type pending in K8:K14 in one entry, beside the six checks still to come.
  4. One number says whether everything ties: the sum of every check’s size, so a +5 and a -5 can’t cancel. The flag under it already reads OK or CHECK (3.6.4). The roll-up in C44: =SUMPRODUCT(ABS(C6:J27)), which reads zero.
  5. On the Cover, C8 =Checks!C44, so the sum of the differences sits under the flag.
  6. Does it tie? Watch FY25 cash on the BS typed over: the cash tie leaves zero and the Cover flag turns to CHECK.
