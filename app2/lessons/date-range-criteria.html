Lesson 124: Date-range criteria: SUMIFS between two dates

Data and Lookups · Summaries from raw rows · medium · Paid

"Revenue in the last seven days" is a SUMIFS with two conditions on the same column: on or after a start date, and on or before an end date, written ">="&C48 with the comparison in quotes and the cell joined by &. SUMPRODUCT does the same the old way, and you will meet it in inherited models. Build a window block with a start and an end date a buyer can type. The key is &.

What you will do

  1. On Summary, type the window’s dates in blue: C48 the start, 9/23/2026, and C49 the end, 9/29/2026, the export’s last day.
  2. A criteria can be an operator joined to a cell: ">="&C48 reads on or after whatever date C48 holds. Two pairs on the same date column make a window, and moving either date moves the answer. Washes in the window in C50: =SUMIFS(Export!$E$5:$E$94,Export!$A$5:$A$94,">="&C48,Export!$A$5:$A$94,"<="&C49).
  3. Retail revenue in the window in C51: the same SUMIFS on column F of the export.
  4. In D50, the old way: =SUMPRODUCT((Export!A5:A94>=C48)*(Export!A5:A94<=C49)*Export!E5:E94), and read that it equals C50.
  5. Clear D50, then answer question 8 on Q&A: E12 Answered and F12 =Summary!C51.
  6. Does it tie? Watch the end date in C49 move back a day: C50 and C51 both fall, and so does the answer on the log.
