Lesson 59: Grouping and outline levels

Formatting and presentation · Alignment and structure · medium · Paid

The site-cost detail is seven rows a buyer wants on demand, not on the page. Group them and an outline button appears in the margin: press it and the seven rows fold to the total; press it again and they’re back. The memo block folds the same way, and groups nest: a second level over revenue to head office folds the page to its answer. The key is Alt+Shift+→.

What you will do

  1. A group puts a button in the margin that folds its rows away and brings them back, and the rows never stop being there. Select the rows whole with Shift+Space and group them with Alt+Shift+→. Select rows 13:19 whole, chemicals and water to marketing, and group them with Alt+Shift+→.
  2. Hide Detail (Alt, A, H) folds the group the active cell sits in; Show Detail (Alt, A, J) opens it again. The outline button in the margin does the same with one click. Fold the detail with Hide Detail, Alt, A, H.
  3. Land on total site costs in B20 and read the page: site costs are one line now.
  4. Open the rows back up from the total with Show Detail, Alt, A, J.
  5. Group the memo block, rows 33:35, the same way, so sites, washes and revenue per wash fold behind their own button.
  6. Group rows that already hold a group and the inner one goes a level deeper: the detail is level 2, the whole block level 1, and each folds on its own. Add a second level: select rows 7:23, retail wash revenue to head office, and group them over the detail with Alt+Shift+→.
  7. Fold the outer level from row 7 with Hide Detail, Alt, A, H, so the P&L reads as its timeline and EBITDA.
  8. Open it again with Show Detail, Alt, A, J, and every line is back.
  9. Does it tie? Watch the detail fold, C13 change to -4500 through the fold, and C24 answer.
