Lesson 38: Your first formulas

Foundations · Basic formulas · medium · Free

A formula starts with = and stays alive: change an input and the answer follows. In this lesson you build Monday’s sales-per-unit, total the week with +, and compute growth with parentheses. Live formulas are the whole point of a spreadsheet.

What you will do

  1. A formula starts with = and recalculates whenever its inputs change. In D3, enter =B3/C3: Monday’s sales per unit.
  2. The arithmetic operators are + - * / with parentheses to group. In B9, add the five days with =B3+B4+B5+B6+B7.
  3. Do the same for Units in C9.
  4. In D9, compute the week’s sales per unit from the two totals.
  5. In B10, compute Friday’s growth over Monday with =(B7-B3)/B3.
