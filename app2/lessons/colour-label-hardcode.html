Lesson 4: Color, label, one hardcode per cell

Foundations · Open and set up · medium · Free

undefined

What you will do

  1. The inputs scratch needs an owner’s pass: move to the Inputs sheet.
  2. Type and press Enter to commit; every pack page states its currency once, near the top. Add the units line: type USD unless stated into A2.
  3. House rule: anything typed is blue, anything calculated stays black, so a reviewer sees the drivers at a glance. Color the typed figures blue — the margin target B5 and the kWh estimate B6.
  4. The wholesale price deserves its own cell: type 0.13 into B4 and color it blue too.
  5. Label where the number comes from: per utility contract in C4.
  6. F2 opens the cell for editing with the caret at the end — read a formula before you change it. B14 still hides the price in =B6*0.13: rewrite it as =B6*B4, so the price lives in one place.
