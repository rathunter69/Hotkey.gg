Lesson 3: The Ribbon by keyboard

Foundations · Open and set up · easy · Free

undefined

What you will do

  1. Alt shows KeyTips: every tab and command has a letter, and Esc backs out one level at a time. Open the Ribbon with Alt, step into Home › Font, then back all the way out with Esc.
  2. The tab letters never change: H Home, N Insert, P Page Layout, M Formulas, A Data, W View. Walk to the View tab with Alt, W, look at what lives there, and leave with Esc.
  3. View › Show › Gridlines (Alt, W, V, G) toggles the grid on the active sheet only. Report is a page someone reads: turn its gridlines off.
  4. Ctrl+1 opens Format Cells from anywhere — the dialog behind most formatting. Open Format Cells with Ctrl+1, glance at its tabs, and close it with Esc.
  5. Every dialog also has a Ribbon route — Format Cells is Home › Format › Format Cells, Alt, H, O, E. Open the same dialog the long way, Alt, H, O, E, and close it again.
