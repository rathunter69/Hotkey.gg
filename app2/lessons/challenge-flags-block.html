Lesson 84: Challenge: the flags block

Formulas and functions · Logic · hard · Paid

Another cluster’s site block for Sep 15: flag it, count it, put the bonus in IFS, add the two compound flags, catch the new site’s error and wire in the override column.

What you will do

  1. Flag each site on target as 1 or 0 in F5:F10 and count the sites on target in F12.
  2. Put the manager bonus in I5:I10 with IFS on the tiers in J5:K8: $50 at 250 washes, $100 at 300 and $150 at 350.
  3. Flag Concern in N5:N10 where a site is below target and over two years old, and a dash where it is not.
  4. Flag Visit in O5:O10 where a site is below target or under 110 cars an hour on Sites, and a dash where it is not.
  5. Catch the new site’s #DIV/0! in revenue per wash, Q5:Q10, with IFERROR and a 0 fallback.
  6. Wire the override: S5:S10 reads R5:R10 when a number is typed there and the washes in C5:C10 when not.
