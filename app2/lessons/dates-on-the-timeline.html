Lesson 43: Dates on the timeline

Formatting and presentation · Number formats · easy · Paid

undefined

What you will do

  1. The year columns B4:D4 are labelled 2024, 2025, 2026: type FY24A, FY25A and FY26E over them with Tab between, ending on E4.
  2. A date is a serial number of days, and Ctrl+1 then D dresses it as Jan-26: the number underneath still sorts, subtracts and feeds a formula. The month headers E4:P4 are bare serials: select them with Ctrl+Shift+→ and dress them as dates, Ctrl+1 then D.
  3. Headers sit over their numbers: from B4 select the row to P4 and right-align it with Alt H A R.
  4. The whole header row A4:P4 is the timeline: select it from A4 and make it bold with Ctrl+B.
  5. The Monthly page heads its columns with the same serials: Go To Monthly!B4, select to M4 and dress them as dates.
  6. Is it a number? Watch P4 drop its dress with Ctrl+1 G, show the serial 46387 underneath, and take the dress back with Ctrl+1 D.
