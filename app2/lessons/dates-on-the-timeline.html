Lesson 44: Dates on the timeline

Formatting and presentation · Number formats · easy · Paid

undefined

What you will do

  1. A date is a serial number of days shown in a date format: 12/31/2024 is 45657 underneath, so it sorts, subtracts and rolls forward. Type the three year ends into C4:E4 as a Tab run: 12/31/2024, 12/31/2025 and 12/31/2026.
  2. Select C4:E4 and show the dates the way the page reads them with Ctrl+1, Date, mmm-yy, which gives Dec-24.
  3. Label the row: type Fiscal year ending in B4 and right-align it over the dates with Alt H A R.
  4. Type the flags A, A and E into C5:E5 as a Tab run, one under each year.
  5. Select the flags C5:E5, right-align them with Alt H A R, then set them in italic with Ctrl+I.
  6. Bold the timeline: select row 4 with Shift+Space and press Ctrl+B.
  7. Does it tie? Watch C4 change to 12/31/2023 and the header read Dec-23: the page rolls with one edit.
