Lesson 60: Hiding vs grouping vs a separate sheet

Formatting and presentation · Alignment and structure · medium · Paid

Three ways to get detail out of the way, and only one is right for each case. Hidden rows vanish and get forgotten: a reader who finds one wonders what else is hidden. Grouped rows fold and show a button, right for detail that belongs on the page. A separate sheet is right when the detail is a different page: the cluster block belongs on its own sheet, not under the P&L. The key is Alt H O U.

What you will do

  1. A hidden row leaves no button and no trace, so a reader who finds one wonders what else is hidden. Select across the gap, whole rows, and Unhide Rows is Alt, H, O, U, O. Someone hid rows 26:30: select rows 25:31 whole and unhide them with Alt, H, O, U, O.
  2. Group the margins block, rows 26:30, instead, so it folds behind a button rather than vanishing.
  3. The cluster block is a different page, so it gets its own sheet rather than a fold under the P&L. Shift+F11 inserts a sheet in front of the active one, and Alt, H, O, R renames it. Go to Print, insert a sheet with Shift+F11 and name it Monthly detail with Alt, H, O, R.
  4. Move Monthly detail to the end with Alt, H, O, M and (move to end), so the working sheet sits behind the pages.
  5. Cut the stray block A41:O58 off the P&L with Ctrl+X and paste it onto Monthly detail at A1.
  6. Does it tie? Watch P&L!C7 change to 18500, and C24 answer with nothing hidden.
