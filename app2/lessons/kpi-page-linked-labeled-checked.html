Lesson 134: The KPI page: linked, labeled, checked

Data and Lookups · Summaries from raw rows · medium · Paid

The blocks are built; now they become a page a buyer reads: a title from Inputs, a source line, and a checks block that ties the cubes to the export and the KPI ratios to their parts, with one flag over the lot. Everything on the page is a link or a formula; nothing is typed but the inputs. Then the print set-up, so the page leaves the building on one sheet of paper. The key is Ctrl+1.

What you will do

  1. On Summary, make the title in A1 a formula on the export period, ="Clearcoat Express: KPI page, "&Inputs!$C$14, in green.
  2. In B69, type a source line that names Export, Lists and the six site tabs, and set it in italics with Ctrl+I.
  3. A KPI page proves itself: every ratio ties back to its parts and every block to the export, each as a difference that reads zero, and one flag over the checks says whether the page ties. A reviewer reads the flag first. Finish the checks: C74 =ROUND(M11*H11-L11,0), C75 =H11-F21, and the flag in C77 over all five.
  4. Print set-up: landscape (Alt, P, O, L), then Page Setup, Fit to one page wide by one tall.
  5. Repeat rows 1:4 on every page (Alt, P, I), and put the file name left and the date right in the footer.
  6. Does it tie? Watch an export row change: the cubes, the KPI block and the window all move, and the flag in C77 stays at zero.
