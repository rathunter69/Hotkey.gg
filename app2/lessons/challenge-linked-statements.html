Lesson 182: Challenge: schedules linked into balanced statements

Finance and Accounting · Linking the statements · hard · Paid

Schedules done, ten projected links missing across the statements and the revolver, on fresh inputs. Link them, wire the revolver and get the check to zero in every year.

What you will do

  1. On the IS, depreciation in F26:J26, interest in F28:J28 and tax in F30:J30 from their schedules.
  2. On the CF, depreciation added back in F7:J7 and capex in F14:J14.
  3. On the BS, cash in F6:J6 from the CF’s closing cash and PP&E in F9:J9 from its roll-forward.
  4. The revolver’s draw in F101:J101 and repayment in F102:J102 on Schedules.
  5. The balance check in F28:J28 of BS, reading zero in every year.
  6. Go to the checks flag in C7 of Cover, reading OK.
