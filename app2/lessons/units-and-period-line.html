Lesson 60: A units line that writes itself

Formatting and presentation · Dates and text for presentation · medium · Paid

undefined

What you will do

  1. Text in quotes rides along with the cells it joins: B4 is the currency, " " the space between, B5 the units, and the rest of the sentence after them. In Inputs!B17 enter =B4&" "&B5&" unless stated; costs shown as negatives" and read the units line.
  2. Point P&L!A2 at it with =Inputs!B17, so the typed units line goes.
  3. Do the same in Monthly!A2: =Inputs!B17.
  4. In Print!A2 enter =Inputs!B17 and set it in italic with Ctrl+I, like the other two.
  5. On Inputs, type millions into B5, read the three units lines, then put thousands back with Ctrl+Z.
  6. Does it tie? Watch Inputs!B4 change to EUR, and the units line on all three pages change with it.
