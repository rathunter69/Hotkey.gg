Lesson 70: Dynamic titles with &

Formatting and presentation · Dates and text for presentation · medium · Paid

The & joins text to text, so a title can be built from cells: the company name on Inputs, the statement name, the period from the timeline. Change the name once and every page’s title follows. Build the P&L, Monthly and Print titles from Inputs, and a period line that reads the first and last year ends. The key is &.

What you will do

  1. A title typed on every page goes wrong one page at a time. Built with &, it reads the company name from the one cell on Inputs, and the formats on A1 stay as they were. In P&L!A1 join Inputs!B3 to the words Historical Financials with &, and see the title still center across A1:E1.
  2. In Monthly!A1 join Inputs!B3 to the words FY26 by month with & in the same way.
  3. Copy P&L!A1 and paste its formats onto Print!A1 with Ctrl+Alt+V, T.
  4. In Print!A1 join Inputs!B3 to the words Summary financials with &.
  5. TEXT with yyyy writes a year end as its year, and & strings the words around it, so the line can never name the wrong years. In Print!B4 build the line Fiscal years 2024 to 2026 with & from TEXT('P&L'!C4,"yyyy") and TEXT('P&L'!E4,"yyyy").
  6. Make B4 bold and color it green, since it reads the P&L.
  7. Read B4 back with F2, follow its pieces, and leave it with Esc.
  8. Does it tie? Watch Inputs!B3 change to Clearcoat Express Holdings, and three titles change with it.
