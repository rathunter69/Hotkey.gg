Lesson 19: Insert and delete rows and columns

Foundations · Rows, columns and sheets · medium · Free

A report grows: a day gets split, a column gets added, a stale row goes. In this lesson you insert and delete whole rows and columns with Ctrl+Shift+= and Ctrl+-, and watch the Total formula follow the move. Structure edits that keep the formulas honest are a core analyst skill.

What you will do

  1. Ctrl+Shift+= inserts and Ctrl+- deletes the selected whole rows or columns. Select row 5 with Shift+Space and insert a blank row above Wednesday with Ctrl+Shift+=.
  2. The split is off again: select the new row 5 and delete it with Ctrl+-.
  3. Insert a column before Sales from the Ribbon: land on B3 and press Alt, H, I, C.
  4. Take the empty column out again with Alt, H, D, C.
