Lesson 137: Challenge: a KPI block that ties to the POS data

Data and Lookups · Summaries from raw rows · hard · Paid

A fresh export, and the KPI page has lost its ratios, its washes cube, the window and two checks. Rebuild them until every check reads zero, and answer question 9 on the log.

What you will do

  1. Utilization by site in Summary!I5:I10: washes over daily capacity times days reported.
  2. Member share by site in M5:M10: member washes over washes.
  3. Washes in the window in C50: a SUMIFS between the dates in C48 and C49.
  4. The washes cube C15:E20: one SUMIFS on the site $B15 and the week C$14, entered with Ctrl+Enter.
  5. Tie both cubes to the export in C72 and C73, so the flag in C77 reads zero.
  6. Answer question 9 on Q&A: E9 Answered, and F9 a link to the washes over the export.
