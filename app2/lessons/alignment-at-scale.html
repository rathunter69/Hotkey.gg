Lesson 58: Wrap, indent, Center Across Selection at scale

Formatting and presentation · Alignment and structure · medium · Paid

Alignment is the same three moves you know, done on a whole page at once: headers right over figures, sub-lines indented, long headers wrapped rather than widened, titles centered across the block. The Monthly page has twelve month headers, a full year, two levels of lines and a title over fifteen columns. Finish its alignment in a handful of presses. The key is Alt H W.

What you will do

  1. A header sits bold and right-aligned over its figures, so the eye reads straight down a column. The months are already right-aligned; one Ctrl+B on the row makes the whole header read as one. On Monthly, bold the whole header row B4:O4 with one Ctrl+B.
  2. Retype the capitals in the full-year header O4 as Full year, in sentence case.
  3. A long header wraps inside its column rather than widening it, so every period column keeps the same width. Wrap Text is Alt, H, W. Wrap O4 with Alt, H, W rather than widening column O.
  4. Set the units line in A2 italic with Ctrl+I, left and unwrapped, as it reads on the P&L.
  5. The label column now says the hierarchy without a word added: a bold section, its lines one level in, and a bold total with its top border. The indents came across with the P&L’s formats in the last lesson. Land on Labor in B14 and read the levels down column B: section, line and total.
  6. Does it tie? Watch Monthly!C7 change to 2000, and the full year in O10 answer.
