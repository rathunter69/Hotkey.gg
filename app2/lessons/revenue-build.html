Lesson 170: The revenue build: sites × washes × days × ticket, plus members × fee

Finance and Accounting · Schedules · hard · Paid

Revenue is built from its drivers, never typed: sites from the rollout, washes a day per site, days in the year and the retail ticket, plus members times the fee. Every driver sits on Inputs and every line on Schedules is a formula, one a row, with the actual years reading Data through the projection flag. Build the rollout and the revenue build, then tie revenue on Checks. The key is =.

What you will do

  1. A corkscrew rolls a balance: opening, plus what comes in, less what goes out, is the closing, and the closing is next year’s opening. The rollout rolls this way, and so will PP&E, the debt and equity. One formula a row carries FY24 to FY31: the flag reads Data in an actual year and the drivers in a projected one. Build the rollout in C6:J9 as a corkscrew: opening sites, new sites, closures and closing sites, each through the flag.
  2. Average sites in the year in C10:J10: the average of the opening and the closing count.
  3. A driver-based build multiplies inputs a buyer can question: (mature sites × 250 + new sites × 200) × 365 is a year of washes. The model is in thousands, so the line divides by the labeled 1,000 on Inputs and never by a 1000 typed into the formula. Washes a day for a mature and a new site in C13:J14, then a year of washes, in thousands, in C15:J15.
  4. Split the washes in C16:J19: washes a day per average site, the member share, retail washes and member washes.
  5. The retail ticket, its growth and retail revenue in C20:J22: the ticket grows off the prior year by the live driver.
  6. Average members, membership revenue and other revenue in C23:J25.
  7. Total revenue and revenue per wash in C26:J27; FY26 reads $13.89, the figure on the Chapter 2 P&L.
  8. The actual years read zero now. The projected years show the whole build until the income statement is linked to it in the next module, and then they read zero too. On Checks, tie the income statement’s revenue to the build in C10:J10 with a ROUND around the difference.
  9. Does it tie? Watch two more sites go into FY28 on the Base case row of Inputs, and revenue answer from FY28 on.
