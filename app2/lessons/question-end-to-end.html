Lesson 135: A buyer’s question answered end to end

Data and Lookups · Summaries from raw rows · hard · Paid

Sponsor C’s question 7 asks for revenue per site per open hour in the last week, and which site led. Decide the cut, then build it from the pieces you have: the date window, the hours from the KPI block, SUMIFS and RANK. Then write the answer into the log as a formula on the cells, with the status set to Answered. That is the loop for every question in the room. The key is =.

What you will do

  1. Every question in the log goes the same way: read it, decide the cut (here, the window’s seven days by site), build the cut from the blocks already on the page, and answer with a link, never a typed figure. On Summary, the block’s sites in B55:B60 read the site block: =$B5 with Ctrl+Enter.
  2. Revenue in the window by site in C55:C60: a SUMIFS on the site and both date criteria, the dates anchored.
  3. Open hours in the window in D55:D60: hours a day from the KPI block times the days, =E5*($C$49-$C$48+1).
  4. Revenue an hour in E55:E60 =C55/D55, and its rank in F55:F60 =RANK(E55,$E$55:$E$60).
  5. The leader in C61, =INDEX($B$55:$B$60,MATCH(1,$F$55:$F$60,0)), and its figure in D61 the same way on column E.
  6. On Q&A, question 7: E11 Answered, and F11 a formula on the leader and its figure in dollars an hour.
  7. Does it tie? Watch the window in C48:C49 move a week back, and the answer in F11 on the log follow it.
