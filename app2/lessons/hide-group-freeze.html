Lesson 19: Hide, group, freeze

Foundations · Structure · medium · Free

undefined

What you will do

  1. Ctrl+0 hides the selected columns and Ctrl+9 the rows; Ctrl+Shift+) and Ctrl+Shift+( unhide whatever is hidden inside the selection. Energy cost and Gross profit are working columns: select the whole of E:F from the header row and hide them with Ctrl+0.
  2. A buyer’s analyst will find a hidden column and wonder what else is hidden: select D:G across the gap and unhide with Ctrl+Shift+).
  3. Rows hide the same way: select the whole daily table, rows 13:21, hide them with Ctrl+9, then bring them back with Ctrl+Shift+(.
  4. Alt+Shift+→ groups the selected whole columns or rows into an outline with a button to fold and unfold; Alt+Shift+← ungroups them. Group, don’t hide: select the whole of E:F again and group the two working columns with Alt+Shift+→.
  5. Fold the group with Hide Detail, Alt A H: the two columns tuck behind an outline button instead of vanishing.
  6. Unfold it with Show Detail, Alt A J: the workings are there when a reader wants them, and the group stays.
  7. Freeze Panes (Alt W F F) pins every row above and every column left of the active cell so they stay in view as the sheet scrolls. The title, units, header row and site column must stay in view: land on B5 and freeze panes there with Alt W F F.
  8. Does it tie? Watch Cedar Park’s kWh in C9 change to 3,000 and the Total in C11 answer, with the heads frozen in place.
