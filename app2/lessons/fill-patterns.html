Lesson 165: The fill patterns: anchor, fill right, AutoSum a block, F4

Finance and Accounting · Model setup and efficiencies · medium · Paid

Every skill here is Chapter 1’s: anchors (1.6.3), Ctrl+R (1.6.5), AutoSum over a block (1.6.2) and F4 to repeat (1.5.4), done as one motion across a block the size of a model. Write the first-period formula with its anchors right, select to the last period, Ctrl+R, then the next row. A row of eight is one formula; a block of forty rows is forty formulas and forty fills, and it takes minutes, not an afternoon. Practice on the cost build’s skeleton. The key is Ctrl+R.

What you will do

  1. The labor line’s first-period formula is written with its anchors set: the inputs on Inputs fixed with $, the timeline and the row’s own cells relative. Get the first cell right and the fill does the rest. On Schedules, fill labor’s formula in C32 right to J32: Shift+→ to the last period, then Ctrl+R.
  2. Ctrl+R fills every row of a selection from its own first cell, so five rows with their first periods written take one motion, not five. The other five lines in one motion: select C33:J37 and Ctrl+R.
  3. Alt+= on a block with an empty row beneath writes a SUM under every column at once (1.6.2). Total the block in row 38 with one Alt+= over C32:J38.
  4. The desk format (2.2.1): thousands with a comma, negatives in brackets, a zero as a dash, and the spaces that keep the figures lined up with the brackets. Format C32:J32 with the custom code #,##0_);(#,##0);"-"_) from Ctrl+1.
  5. F4 outside a formula repeats the last action whole, so the format code goes onto the next block without the dialog. A one-press route to a custom format exists only as a button you add yourself. Select C33:J37 and press F4 to repeat the format.
  6. Does it tie? Watch FY24’s labor on Data rise by 400: C32 reads it, and the total in C38 answers.
