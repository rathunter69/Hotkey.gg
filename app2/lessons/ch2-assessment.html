Lesson 55: Assessment: the section on fresh figures

Formatting and presentation · Project and assessment · hard · Paid

undefined

What you will do

  1. On the P&L give C7:E24 the desk number format, $ on rows 7, 10 and 24, and the memo rows 33 to 35 their own codes.
  2. Turn the costs in rows 13 to 19 and 23 negative with Paste Special Multiply, then make C22 and C24 additions filled to E.
  3. Head the Margins and growth block at B26, four margin lines in C27:E30 in italic percent, and the CAGR in F30.
  4. Link C4 to Inputs B6 and step D4:E4 a year with EOMONTH, FY codes in green, the A, A and E flags in row 5.
  5. On Inputs give B12:B16 their unit codes, then the units line in B17, the next update in B18 and the headline in B19.
  6. Give the P&L its anatomy: clear column A, title and units line from Inputs, sections bold, lines indented, totals ruled, inputs blue.
  7. Draw the A/E divider down D4:D35, then retype the labels B7:B35 in sentence case with the source and footnote in B36:B37.
  8. Set the P&L widths, the title row to 24, freeze panes at C5, and group rows 13 to 19, 26 to 30 and 33 to 35.
  9. Bring Monthly to the P&L: labels into B6:B30, costs negative, rows 22 and 24 as additions, the margins block and Paste Formats.
  10. Head Monthly: title and units line from Inputs, month ends in D4:N4 by EOMONTH, C4 blue, month names in C5:N5 by TEXT.
  11. Finish Monthly with its source in B31, two checks against the P&L in C34:C35, the P&L widths, panes at C5 and gridlines off.
  12. Type the Go to column in Q4:Q7, then name Monthly rows 10, 20 and 24 as Rev, SiteCosts and EBITDA.
  13. Add the rules: Monthly C10:N10 yellow below 3,000, the P&L checks C39:C40 light red and stopping, negative margins in red text.
  14. Build Print from links: title, period line and FY heads, six lines pointed at the P&L, formats, its check, divider and layout.
  15. In Page Setup, Alt, P, S, P, set the pack landscape, one page wide by two tall, rows 1:5 repeated, the three-part footer, centered.
  16. Does it tie? Change FY25A retail wash revenue on the P&L and watch Print C5 answer while every check stays at zero.
