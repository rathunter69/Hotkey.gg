Lesson 87: Period keys: a month and a quarter from every date

Formulas and functions · Dates · medium · Paid

Ninety transactions are useful once they can be grouped, and grouping needs a key: a column that says which month or quarter each row belongs to. TEXT(A5,"yyyy-mm") writes 2026-09 as text you can count on; EOMONTH(A5,0) gives the month end as a date you can sort on; a quarter is ROUNDUP(MONTH(A5)/3,0). Add the keys to Transactions, and module 3.3 counts and sums on them. The key is TEXT.

What you will do

  1. TEXT(value, "format") writes a number through a format code as text: the date stays a date in A, and G holds the label 2026-09, which sorts in order. Go to Transactions and give G5 the month key as text: =TEXT(A5,"yyyy-mm"), then Enter.
  2. Key every wash by month: Go To G94, Ctrl+Shift+↑ up to G5 and Ctrl+D, and all ninety rows read 2026-09.
  3. EOMONTH(date, 0) is the last day of the date’s own month; EOMONTH(date, 1) of the next. Its sibling EDATE(date, n) keeps the day and moves n months. The month end in H5 is =EOMONTH(A5,0), a real date shown as mmm-yy that sorts and subtracts; Enter.
  4. Fill the month end down: Ctrl+↓ on the month keys finds row 94, then → and Ctrl+Shift+↑ select H5:H94 for Ctrl+D.
  5. ROUNDUP(x, 0) rounds up to the next whole number, so months 7 to 9 over 3 give quarter 3. The desk’s other test: MOD(MONTH(A5),3)=0 is TRUE in a quarter-end month. The quarter number in I5 is =ROUNDUP(MONTH(A5)/3,0): September is month 9, so quarter 3; Tab on.
  6. & joins text and values into one string: "Q" and the quarter number and a space and the year read Q3 2026. Label the quarter in J5 from I5: ="Q"&I5&" "&YEAR(A5) reads Q3 2026; Enter.
  7. Fill the quarter and its label down together: find row 94 from the month end, select I5:J94 and Ctrl+D.
  8. WEEKDAY(date, 2) numbers Monday as 1 through Sunday as 7, so the date less its weekday, plus one, is that week’s Monday. The week key in K5 is the Monday of the week, =A5-WEEKDAY(A5,2)+1; fill it to K94 the same way.
  9. Does it tie? Watch the first wash move to 10/5/2026: its month key reads 2026-10, its quarter Q4 2026 and its week that Monday.
