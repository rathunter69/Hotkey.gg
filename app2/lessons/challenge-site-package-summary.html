Lesson 58: Challenge: the export rolled up to a site × package summary

Formulas and functions · Math and aggregation · hard · Paid

undefined

What you will do

  1. Count the washes by site in C15:C20 with COUNTIF on Transactions!$B$5:$B$94, and total them in C21 with Alt+=.
  2. The average retail ticket by site in G15:G20: AVERAGEIFS of the amounts on the site, with the amounts ">0".
  3. Rank the sites by washes in K15:K20 with =RANK(C15,$C$15:$C$20).
  4. Fill the site by package block: washes with COUNTIFS in C41:E46, retail revenue with SUMIFS in F41:H46.
  5. Retail washes by package in D25:D27, C29 =SUMPRODUCT(D25:D27,E25:E27), and the blended ticket in C30 =C29/C21.
  6. Two managers over-counted: type the adjustments in G53 and G55 that bring the check in I51:I56 to zero, in blue.
