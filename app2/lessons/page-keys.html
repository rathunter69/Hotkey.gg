Lesson 9: Pages and screens

Foundations · Moving · easy · Free

A real log runs past the bottom of the screen, and holding an arrow down is not a plan. In this lesson you move through a 60-day sales log a screen at a time with PgDn, PgUp and Alt+PgDn. Paging is how you skim a long sheet without losing your place.

What you will do

  1. PgDn and PgUp move one screen down and up; Alt+PgDn and Alt+PgUp one screen right and left. Page down from the top until the active cell has left the first screen.
  2. Page one screen to the right with Alt+PgDn.
  3. Page back up until the active cell is inside the first ten rows.
  4. Jump straight to B63, the Total at the bottom, with Ctrl+End.
