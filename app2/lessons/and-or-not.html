Lesson 54: AND, OR and NOT

Formulas and functions · Logic · medium · Paid

undefined

What you will do

  1. Site age is the years since a site opened. A new site takes about two years to ramp up to its volume, so a buyer reads the young ones separately. Select the site ages L5:L10, typed until module 3.2 builds them from the opening dates: Cedar Park is a week old and reads 0.
  2. AND(test, test) is TRUE only when every test inside it holds. Written bare it shows TRUE or FALSE, so you can read the answer before you wrap it in IF. In M5, type =AND(C5<D5,L5>2) with no IF around it, fill it down to M10 and read the TRUE and FALSE column.
  3. Wrap it for the page: N5 =IF(AND(C5<D5,L5>2),"Concern","-"), filled down to N10.
  4. OR(test, test) is TRUE when any one test holds, so one IF covers two reasons to send someone out. A site needs a visit if it is below target OR under 110 cars an hour: O5 =IF(OR(C5<D5,Sites!F5<110),"Visit","-"), filled down.
  5. NOT(test) flips TRUE to FALSE and back: NOT(L5>2) is TRUE for a site two years old or younger. The ramping sites are the ones NOT over two years old: P5 =IF(NOT(L5>2),"Ramping","-"), filled down to P10.
  6. Open Riverside’s Visit in O7 with F2 and read the OR, then Esc: it is on target, so the 100 cars an hour on Sites carried it.
  7. Does it tie? Watch Cedar Park’s age in L10 change to 3 and its flags move: Ramping goes, and Concern comes up in N10.
