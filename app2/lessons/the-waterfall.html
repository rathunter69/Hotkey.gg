Lesson 202: From enterprise value to the owners’ proceeds: the waterfall

Valuation · The bids and the waterfall · hard · Paid

Enterprise value is what the buyer pays for the business; the owners’ proceeds are what is left once everyone ahead of them is paid. Net debt is repaid, the advisers’ and lawyers’ fees come off, the management option pool takes its share of the equity, and the rest is split by ownership: two founders at 35% each, the family office at 30%. That’s the waterfall, one line per claim. Build it for bid A, then fill it across the three. The key is =.

What you will do

  1. The waterfall pays claims in the order they rank: net debt first, then the fees, then the option pool, the owners last. Each deduction is a MIN of the claim and what is left above it, so a price below the claims floors the owners at zero instead of showing a negative. Start bid A’s waterfall in C30:C32: enterprise value from the priced value, less net debt as a MIN, then equity value.
  2. Fees are 2% of enterprise value, the advisers’ and the lawyers’. The option pool takes 5% of the equity above the strike the options were set at, MAX floors that at zero, and MIN stops it taking more than the fees left. Take off the fees in C33 and the option pool in C34, each a MIN of the claim and what is left above it.
  3. What is left is the net proceeds to the owners, pre-tax: the structure changes what each keeps after tax, and that is the tax adviser’s question. Each owner takes their share from the ownership table. Total the net proceeds in C35, then split them in C36:C38 by the ownership in C54:C56.
  4. Check the split in C39: =ROUND(SUM(C36:C38)-C35,2), which reads zero.
  5. Every line in bid A’s column reads its own column’s priced value and the anchored inputs, so one Ctrl+R over the block writes bids B and C. Select C30:E39 and fill bid A’s waterfall across to bids B and C with Ctrl+R.
  6. Give the three deductions the desk format #,##0_);(#,##0);"-"_): rows 31, 33 and 34, the last two with F4.
  7. Give the proceeds line, C35:E35, its double bottom border with Alt, H, B, B.
  8. Does it tie? Watch the fees in C49 go from 2% to 3%: every owner’s line under every bid falls.
