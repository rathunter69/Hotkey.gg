Lesson 1: Welcome: the feed in sixty seconds

Foundations · Welcome · easy · Free

undefined

What you will do

  1. The arrow keys move the active cell one cell per press — feel how long 60 rows takes. Round one, the slow way: walk down the dates with ↓ until you reach the last one, A61.
  2. Ctrl+Home returns to A1, and Ctrl+Arrow jumps to the edge of the data in one press. Round two: back to the top with Ctrl+Home, then the same trip in one Ctrl+↓.
  3. Shift+Arrow grows a selection one cell per press. Now select: from the first Revenue figure E2, take one site’s twelve days with Shift+↓.
  4. Ctrl+Shift+Arrow selects to the edge of the data in one press. The fast way: Ctrl+↑ to the top of the column, then the whole of Revenue in one Ctrl+Shift+↓.
  5. Finish where every job starts: Ctrl+Home, back to A1.
