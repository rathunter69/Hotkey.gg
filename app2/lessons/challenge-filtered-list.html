Lesson 130: Challenge: a dump into a filtered, deduplicated list

Data and Lookups · Lists and tables · hard · Paid

A raw dump on Export sort. Sort it two levels, filter Airport and count what shows with SUBTOTAL, rebuild the unique site list and prove it, and put back the case drop-down and the washes limit.

What you will do

  1. Sort Export sort by Site, then by Date oldest to newest, in one Sort dialog.
  2. Filter the copy to AUS-AIR and count the rows showing in J2 with =SUBTOTAL(103,A5:A94).
  3. Copy Export!B4:B94 to Lists!N4 and run Remove Duplicates: the six site codes.
  4. Prove the list in O5:O10 with COUNTIF against the export, and total it in O11: ninety rows.
  5. Make Scenarios!C10 a drop-down of the cases in Lists!$L$5:$L$7.
  6. Limit the washes a day inputs Scenarios!C5:E5 to a whole number from 100 to 600.
