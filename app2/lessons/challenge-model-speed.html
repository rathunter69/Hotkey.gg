Lesson 198: Challenge: the three benchmarks in one run

Finance and Accounting · Model speed · hard · Paid

Fresh Base drivers, and a piece of each benchmark cut back: the rollout and total revenue, the cost build and its format, the debt links on the cash flow. Put all three back before the clock runs out.

What you will do

  1. Fill the rollout C6:J9 on Schedules right, and total revenue in C26:J26 as =C22+C24+C25.
  2. Fill the cost build C30:J43 right in one press.
  3. Give the cost lines C32:J37 the desk number format, #,##0_);(#,##0);"-"_).
  4. Link the debt on the cash flow: =Schedules!C80 into C18:J19, =Schedules!C90 into C20:J21 and the revolver into C24:J24.
  5. Color the new links green: C18:J21 through Font Color, then F4 on C24:J24.
