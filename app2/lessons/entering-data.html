Lesson 10: Entering data

Foundations · Entering and editing · easy · Free

A sheet is only useful once there is data in it. In this lesson you fill in the Weekly Sales Report by typing into cells and confirming each entry with Enter or Tab. Quick, accurate entry is the first thing anyone notices about a good Excel user.

What you will do

  1. Type into a cell, then Enter confirms the entry and moves down, while Esc throws it away. In A1, type Weekly Sales Report and press Enter.
  2. Excel tells numbers from text on its own: numbers line up on the right of the cell, text on the left. Enter the Sales for Monday to Friday in B3:B7: 1200, 950, 1430, 1100, 1675.
  3. Tab confirms the entry and moves right instead of down. Enter 40 as the Monday Units in C3 and confirm it with Tab.
  4. Delete clears the selected cells without opening them. B8 holds the stray word draft: select it and clear it.
