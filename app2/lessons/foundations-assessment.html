Lesson 38: Assessment: Monday morning

Foundations · Project and assessment · hard · Free

undefined

What you will do

  1. A1: Voltline - City Weekly KPI Report, w/c 22 Sep 2026, with the city as Costs A1 names it; A2: USD unless stated.
  2. Row 4, bold: Site, Week, kWh sold, Revenue ($), Energy cost ($), Gross profit ($), Avg price ($/kWh), Margin % across A4:H4.
  3. The five sites from Inputs A9:A13 into A5:A9, and the week label w/c 22 Sep in B5:B9.
  4. kWh sold and revenue C5:D9 as live links to Raw’s site totals I8:J12, pointed across sheets.
  5. Energy cost E5:E9 is kWh times the wholesale price, =C5*Inputs!$B$4 anchored, then color every link C5:E9 green.
  6. F5:F9 =D5-E5, G5:G9 =D5/C5, H5:H9 =F5/D5 filled down; Total in A10, C10:F10 by AutoSum, G10 =D10/C10, H10 =F10/D10.
  7. Daily block: kWh sold by day in A12, Site and Mon–Sat bold in row 13, sites indented in A14:A18, B14:G18 green links to Raw’s by-day block.
  8. Checks in A20, bold; the three labels A21:A23; B21 =D10-Raw!J13, B22 =SUM(C5:C9)-C10, B23 =IF(AND(H10>=0,H10<=1),0,1), all reading 0.
  9. Number formats: C5:F10, B14:G18 and B21:B23 comma 0; G5:G10 currency 2; H5:H10 percent 1; D5:F5 and D10:F10 currency 0.
  10. Style: total row bold, top border; title bold, a size up, centered across A1:H1; A2 italic; C4:H4 wrapped; C4:H4 and B13:G13 right-aligned.
  11. Widths: B:F 12, G:H 14, column A AutoFit to the sites, row 4 AutoFit; freeze panes at B5; gridlines off.
  12. Page setup: landscape, fit to one page, rows 1:4 as print titles, &[File] in the left footer and &[Date] in the right.
  13. Does it tie? Watch the first site’s Monday kWh on Raw change to 3,000 and the Report’s total in C10 answer while the checks stay zero.
