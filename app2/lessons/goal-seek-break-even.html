Lesson 145: Goal Seek: break-even washes per site

Data and Lookups · Scenarios and sensitivity · medium · Paid

Contribution per wash is the ticket less the cost of a wash, what each wash puts toward the site’s fixed costs, and break-even washes are the daily site costs divided by it. You can solve that by hand, and Goal Seek (Alt, A, W, G) solves it by turning a dial: set this cell to that value by changing this input. It writes its answer over the input, so read it, note it, and put the input back. The key is Alt A W G.

What you will do

  1. Domain’s daily site costs from Lists: C41 =INDEX(Lists!$H$5:$H$10,MATCH(C40,Lists!$B$5:$B$10,0)).
  2. Contribution per wash: C42 =G6-Inputs!$C$5-(1-G7)*Inputs!$C$7, the ticket less the wash and the retail costs.
  3. Domain’s daily contribution at 250 washes a day: C44 =C43*C42-C41.
  4. Break-even by hand: C45 =C41/C42, the site costs over the contribution per wash.
  5. Goal Seek (Alt, A, W, G) sets one formula cell to a value by changing one input: Set cell, To value (Alt+V), By changing cell (Alt+C). Enter runs it and Enter again keeps the answer, written over the input. Goal Seek: on C44, set it to 0 by changing C43, and keep the answer.
  6. Note the answer in C46 as 121, beside its label, and put 250 back in C43.
  7. Does it tie? Watch the cost per wash on Inputs go from $1.50 to $2.00 and the break-even in C45 rise.
