Lesson 103: PV, FV and PMT: the site-build loan

Formulas and functions · Time value of money · medium · Paid

undefined

What you will do

  1. On Loans, select the four blue inputs in C6:C9: the principal, the annual rate, the term in years and the payments a year.
  2. A loan is paid per period, so its rate and its term have to be per period too. The rate a month is the annual rate over the payments a year, and the periods are the years times the payments a year, so a reader can change either input and both follow. Turn the terms monthly: =C7/C9 in C12 for the monthly rate, then =C8*C9 in C13 for the number of payments.
  3. PMT(rate, periods, principal) returns the level payment that clears the loan, and it comes back negative because it is cash going out. A minus in front makes the page read a positive payment. PMT, PV and FV also take an optional last argument, type: left out, the payment falls at the end of each month, as a loan’s does; 1 puts it at the start, the way rent is paid. In C16 build the monthly payment as =-PMT(C12,C13,C6), the minus turning the cash out into a positive payment.
  4. In C17 total what the loan costs as =C16*C13, then in C18 take the principal off it, =C17-C6, to read the interest.
  5. PV runs PMT backwards: given the rate, the periods and a payment, it returns the loan that payment clears. The payment goes in as a negative, cash out, so the loan reads positive. Prove the payment with PV: in C21 =PV(C12,C13,-C16) turns the monthly payment back into the $3.5m loan in C6.
  6. Now ask PV a real question: change C21 to =PV(C12,C13,-35000) for the loan a $35,000 monthly payment supports.
  7. FV runs it forwards: FV(rate, periods, payment, present value) is what is left after the last period. With the loan going in as cash out and the payment coming back, it reads the balance after 120 payments. In C22 check the loan clears: =FV(C12,C13,C16,-C6) is the balance after the last payment, and it reads zero.
  8. Put FV to work on growth: change C22 to =FV(C7,C8,0,-1000000), what $1m grows to in ten years at the loan’s annual rate.
  9. Does it tie? Watch the rate in C7 change to 6% and the payment in C16 and the interest in C18 both fall.
