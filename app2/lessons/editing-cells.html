Lesson 51: Editing a cell

Foundations · Entering and editing · easy · Free

Data is rarely right the first time. In this lesson you fix a misspelled title, a wrong figure and a misspelled day in the Weekly Sales Report. Knowing when to retype a cell and when to edit it saves time all day long.

What you will do

  1. F2 opens the cell for editing with the insertion point at the end, and Backspace deletes the character before it. Fix the title in A1: change Reprot to Report.
  2. To replace a cell, select it and type: the old contents go the moment you start. Replace the Tuesday Sales in B4 with 1950.
  3. In Edit mode ← and → move the insertion point, and Home and End jump to either end. Correct Wenesday in A5 to Wednesday by inserting the missing d.
