Lesson 35: A tour of the Home tab

Foundations · The Ribbon and dialogs · easy · Free

The Home tab holds the everyday commands, and every one answers to Alt plus a short letter walk. In this lesson you grow the title, italicise a note, underline a name, and practise backing out of a walk you did not mean to start. The Home letters become reflexes fast because you use them constantly.

What you will do

  1. Grow the title’s font one step with Alt, H, F, G.
  2. Ctrl+B bold, Ctrl+I italic, Ctrl+U underline — and Alt, H, 1 / 2 / 3 from the Ribbon. Italicise the Prepared by note in A10 with Alt, H, 2.
  3. Underline the team name in B10 with Alt, H, 3.
  4. Open the Ribbon with Alt, step into Home, and back all the way out with Esc, changing nothing.
