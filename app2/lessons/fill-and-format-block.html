Lesson 196: Fill and format a block in one pass

Finance and Accounting · Model speed · medium · Paid

The cost build, working capital and PP&E on Schedules hold their FY24 column and nothing else: forty rows waiting to be filled and finished. Fill each block in one motion with Ctrl+R, then format by whole lines: the desk number format, the top border on every total, italic on the margins, each done once and repeated with F4. One pass, no cell touched twice. The key is Ctrl+R.

What you will do

  1. A block fills in one motion: select from its first formula column to FY31 and press Ctrl+R, and every row takes its own first cell’s formula and format. The memo cells in C57 and C67 live in one column only, so the selections stop short of them. Select C30:J56 on Schedules, the cost build and working capital, and fill the block right with Ctrl+R.
  2. Fill the PP&E block the same way: select C60:J66 and press Ctrl+R.
  3. Desk number format on the cost lines C32:J37 through Ctrl+1, then F4 on C41:J41, C47:J48, C53:J55, C61:J62 and C64:J64.
  4. Top border on Site costs C38:J38 with Alt H B P, then F4 on the totals in rows 39, 42, 49, 56, 63 and 65.
  5. Italic on the cost of sales share C31:J31 with Ctrl+I, then F4 on the two margins, C40:J40 and C43:J43.
  6. Does it tie? Watch labor per site, Inputs C50, go from 257 to 300: every cost line moves and the cross-foot in Checks C11 holds at 0.
