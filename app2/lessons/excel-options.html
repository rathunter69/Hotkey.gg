Lesson 25: Options: the settings that matter

Foundations · How Excel works · medium · Free

Some of the settings that matter most in Excel live outside the grid, in Excel Options. In this lesson you open it with Alt, F, T to set calculation to Manual, enable iterative calculation and pin AutoSum to the Quick Access Toolbar, then recalculate with F9. These are the first things an analyst changes on a new machine, because a big model on Automatic recalculates on every keystroke.

What you will do

  1. Alt, F, T opens Excel Options; on its Formulas page M sets Workbook Calculation to Manual, and Enter presses OK. Set Workbook Calculation to Manual.
  2. On the same page I turns on Enable iterative calculation, which lets a model resolve a circular reference such as interest on an average debt balance. Open Excel Options again and enable iterative calculation.
  3. The Quick Access Toolbar sits above the Ribbon: Q opens its page in Options, A adds the highlighted command, and Alt then a number runs it later. Add AutoSum, the first command in the Popular Commands list, to the Quick Access Toolbar.
  4. F9 is Calculate Now: on Manual, it recalculates the whole workbook when you are ready. Recalculate the workbook with F9.
