Lesson 23: Alignment and titles

Foundations · Format · medium · Free

undefined

What you will do

  1. Ctrl+1, then the Alignment tab (A), then Horizontal › Center Across Selection: the title sits centered over the selected columns and every cell stays its own cell. Merge & Center (Alt, H, M, C) looks the same and breaks selection, fill and sorting: the one Ribbon button you’ll learn to avoid. The title in A1 belongs over the page: select A1:I1 and center it across the selection with Ctrl+1, then A, never Merge & Center.
  2. Inside a dialog, Ctrl+PgDn and Ctrl+PgUp (or Ctrl+Tab) move between its tabs; Tab moves between the fields on a tab; Alt plus an underlined letter jumps to one; Space ticks a box; ↑ ↓ pick from a list; Enter is OK and Esc is Cancel. Every dialog in Excel works this way, so learn it on this one. Open Format Cells on A1 again, walk its tabs with Ctrl+PgDn (Number, Alignment, Font, Border, Fill, Protection), then press Esc.
  3. That’s the proof. Across a merged cell the jump stops; across a centered one it runs. Every fill, sort and copy on the page depends on that. Go to A1 and press Ctrl+Shift+→, and notice the selection still sweeps the row in one press, where a merged title would have stopped you.
  4. Alignment is Alt, H, A, then L left, C center, R right. A header sits over its numbers when it’s aligned the way they are: right over figures, left over names. Numbers align right, so their headers should too: select Washes through Prior week rev, C4:I4, and right-align them with Alt, H, A, R.
  5. Italic is for notes, units and footnotes: text that explains the page rather than being part of it. The units line USD unless stated in A2 is a note, not a figure: make it italic with Ctrl+I so it reads as one.
  6. Alt, H, 6 indents a level; Alt, H, 5 takes one back. Indent sub-items with the indent button, never with spaces. Spaces break sorting and lookups later. The eight lines under Washes by day belong to it: jump to A13, select A14:A21 below it and indent them once with Alt, H, 6.
  7. Wrap for labels and notes, never for figures. The row grows to fit and the column stays narrow. On Inputs, the source note per supplier quote in C4 runs past its column: wrap it inside the cell with Alt, H, W.
  8. Alignment rides on the cells; the SUM underneath doesn’t care. Does it tie? Change Cedar Park’s washes in C9 to 300 and watch the Total in C11 answer under the centered title.
