Lesson 51: Formulas across sheets

Foundations · Basic formulas · medium · Free

Real workbooks keep costs on one sheet and the report on another, and the formulas bridge them. In this lesson =Costs!B3 pulls Monday’s costs into the Sales sheet, and a filled margin column reads its own row of the Costs sheet all the way down. Cross-sheet links are how models stay organised.

What you will do

  1. A reference on another sheet names the sheet first: =Costs!B3. In D3, pull Monday’s costs across with =Costs!B3.
  2. In E3, compute Monday’s margin with =B3-Costs!B3.
  3. Select E3:E7 and fill the margin down — each row reads its own Costs row.
