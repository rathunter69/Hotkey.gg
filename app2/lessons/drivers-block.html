Lesson 168: The drivers block: three cases by year, one selector, one live block

Finance and Accounting · Model setup and efficiencies · hard · Paid

A buyer doesn’t want one forecast; they want the company’s case, their own, and the one they can live with, and they want to flip between them without opening a second file. So the drivers, the handful of inputs that move everything, live on Inputs three times, one block per case, by year, and a live block beneath reads whichever case the Cover’s switch names (4.5.1). Every schedule reads the live block and only the live block. The key is CHOOSE.

What you will do

  1. A driver held flat is typed once, in FY27, and each later year points at the one before, in black because it is a formula: one edit rolls through. It is the one accepted link to a link (1.6.4). The Downside’s ticket growth: 0 in F30, then =F30 in G30 filled right to J30.
  2. CHOOSE(Case, …) picks the Management, Base or Downside figure by the Cover’s case number. Case is a name, so it holds still wherever the formula goes, and the three block references move with the fill. The flag hands the actual years their actuals, so history never moves with the switch. Live new sites in C35: =IF(Inputs!C$6=0,Schedules!C7,CHOOSE(Case,C14,C21,C28)), filled right to J35.
  3. Best practice: from 5.3 on, every schedule reads a driver from the live block, never from one of the three cases. A typed input beside a live one is the fault an audit finds first. Live washes a day in C36: =IF(Inputs!C$6=0,Schedules!C16,CHOOSE(Case,C15,C22,C29)), filled right to J36.
  4. Every page says which case it shows (2.6.3), so a printed page can’t be mistaken for another case. The Cover title in A1 takes the case: =Inputs!$C$104&": operating model, "&C5&" case".
  5. A scenario moves several drivers together and tells a story; a sensitivity moves one at a time (4.5.2). The drivers block is for scenarios, the data tables for sensitivities, and a model a buyer trusts has both. The IS title in A1 the same way: =Inputs!$C$104&": income statement, "&Cover!$C$5&" case".
  6. Does it tie? Watch the Cover switch to Downside: the live block moves in FY27 to FY31 and holds in FY24 to FY26.
