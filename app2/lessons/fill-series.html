Lesson 71: Fill Series

Foundations · Copy, paste and fill · easy · Free

Numbering rows by hand is beneath you. In this lesson Fill Series reads the step your first two cells set — 1, 2 and 100, 110 — and continues each run for you, while Ctrl+R shows the difference between filling a series and copying a value. Timelines in every model start exactly like this.

What you will do

  1. Fill Series (Alt, H, F, I, S) continues the step your first two cells set. Select A3:A12 and fill the day numbers 1 to 10 with Fill Series.
  2. Select B3:B12 and fill the targets — the series continues in steps of 10.
  3. Select C2:E2 and fill right with Ctrl+R — a plain fill copies, it does not count.
