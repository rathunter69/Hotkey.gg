Lesson 63: Highlight rules: negatives, exceptions

Formatting and presentation · Conditional formatting · medium · Paid

undefined

What you will do

  1. Select the three margin lines, C27:E29, the cells the first rule will watch.
  2. Alt, H, L opens Conditional Formatting, H the Highlight Cells Rules, and L is Less Than. Type the value, pick the format with the arrows, and Enter adds the rule. Red Text is enough: a margin under zero needs to be noticed, not shouted. Add a rule to C27:E29: Less Than 0, Red Text.
  3. On Monthly, give revenue under 3,800 in C10:N10 a Yellow Fill with Dark Yellow Text, so the slow months show up.
  4. A label that repeats is the easiest error to miss and the first one a buyer finds. Highlight Cells Rules › Duplicate Values (Alt, H, L, H, D) lights every label that appears more than once. Back on the P&L, select the labels B7:B35 and add Duplicate Values with Alt, H, L, H, D, Enter.
  5. B18 and B19 light up because B18 repeats Marketing: type Card fees over it.
  6. Alt, H, L, R opens Manage Rules: every rule on the sheet, its format and the range it applies to, in the order they run. Open Manage Rules with Alt, H, L, R and read the two rules on the P&L, the newest on top, and the ranges they apply to.
  7. Close Manage Rules with Enter, leaving the rules as they are.
  8. Does it tie? Watch January’s retail revenue in Monthly!C7 rise to 2,300, the month reach 4,000, and its yellow clear.
