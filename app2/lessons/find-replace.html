Lesson 29: Find and Replace

Foundations · Entering and editing · medium · Free

You do not hunt a sheet by eye. In this lesson Ctrl+F jumps you to a misspelt day, and Ctrl+H swaps a name and then a whole quarter label everywhere at once. On a real workbook, Replace All is minutes of edits in one keystroke.

What you will do

  1. Find (Ctrl+F) jumps to matching text; Replace (Ctrl+H) swaps it out everywhere. Find the misspelt Wenesday with Ctrl+F and land on it.
  2. With Ctrl+H, replace Sales team with Finance using Replace All.
  3. Replace every Q1 with Q2 in one Replace All — four cells change together.
