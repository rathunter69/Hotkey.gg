Lesson 11: Enter the missing day

Foundations · Enter, edit, copy and fill · easy · Free

undefined

What you will do

  1. Airport’s Saturday never came through: jump to the first blank cell of its row, Raw!C61.
  2. Tab commits the entry and steps one cell right, and Excel remembers where the run began — that is where Enter will return you. Management emailed the day: enter 2,500 kWh in C61 and the price $0.48 in D61, moving right with Tab.
  3. Enter after a Tab run commits and drops to the column the run started in, one row down — where the next record begins. Finish the row with the revenue $1,200.00 in E61 and the energy cost $325.00 in F61, then Enter to land in C62.
  4. Esc throws away an entry you have started: the cell keeps what it had, and nothing is committed. Start retyping the site name over B61, then look again — Airport is right, so discard the entry and keep it.
  5. Five Energy cost cells in column F are blank: select the feed from the top, pick out the blanks with Go To Special and count them.
  6. Ctrl+Enter commits one entry into every selected cell at once — five blanks, one press. Type 0 once and commit it into all five blank Energy cost cells at the same time.
  7. Delete clears what the cell holds and leaves its formatting behind — the italic stays for whatever is typed there next. A stray “draft” note sits in H3, above the site totals block: clear it.
  8. Does it tie? Watch Airport’s Saturday kWh in C61 change to 3,000 and the Airport line of the site totals, I12, answer.
