Lesson 11: Enter the missing day

Foundations · Enter, edit, copy and fill · easy · Free

undefined

What you will do

  1. Airport’s Saturday never came through: jump to the first blank cell of its row, Raw!C61.
  2. Enter commits; with "move selection" off the cursor stays put, which is how you check a figure before you leave it. Enter 250 washes in C61 and press Enter: the cell stays selected, so read it back, then → to D61.
  3. Tab commits and steps one cell right, so a row of figures goes in without an arrow key. The rest of the row as a Tab run: 14 in D61, Tab, 3500 in E61, Tab, 375 in F61, Enter.
  4. AutoComplete proposes an entry from the same column once you have typed enough letters; Esc discards what you started and the cell keeps what it had. Type Airp over B61, AutoComplete offers Airport after three letters, and since it is already right, throw the entry away with Esc.
  5. Five wash-cost cells in column F are blank: select the feed from the top, pick out the blanks with Go To Special and count them.
  6. Ctrl+Enter commits one entry into every selected cell at once, so five blanks take one press. Type 0 once and commit it into all five blank cells of the Wash cost column at the same time.
  7. Delete clears what a cell holds and leaves its format and any note behind. Clear the stray "draft" in H3, then a two-line note in H4: Airport Sat missing, Alt+Enter, emailed manager 9/15.
  8. Two labels on Inputs: USD unless stated into A2, and per supplier quote into C4, beside the cost per wash.
  9. Does it tie? Change Airport’s Saturday washes in C61 to 300 and watch the Airport line of the site totals, I12, answer.
