Lesson 34: The checks row

Foundations · Present and audit · medium · Free

undefined

What you will do

  1. Head a Checks block under the week summary: Checks in A33, bold, then the three check labels in A34:A36 as one Enter run.
  2. A check cell is a live difference between two things that must agree, so it reads 0 when they tie and shows the gap when they do not. In B34, take the five linked sites’ revenue D5:D8 and D10 and subtract the feed’s own total, pointing at Raw’s J13 across sheets.
  3. In B35, the six sites’ kWh must sum to the Total row: enter =SUM(C5:C10)-C11, and it reads 0 while the total row is honest.
  4. In B36, a margin outside 0 to 100% is a mistake somewhere: enter =IF(AND(H11>=0,H11<=1),0,1), which reads 0 while the margin is plausible.
  5. Give the three checks B34:B36 the figures’ format, comma style with no decimals, so all three read a plain 0.
  6. A reviewer will ask what the first check compares: from B34, Ctrl+[ follows the trail to D5, the first revenue figure it reads.
  7. Does it tie? Watch a number typed over Mueller’s revenue link in D6 and the revenue check in B34 leave zero: the break is caught.
