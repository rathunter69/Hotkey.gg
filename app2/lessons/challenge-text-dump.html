Lesson 102: Challenge: a text dump into a usable table

Formulas and functions · Text · hard · Paid

A raw dump from the San Antonio cluster: codes with stray spaces, memos with three facts, amounts as text. Make it a table the formulas can read, and bring the checks to zero.

What you will do

  1. Clean the codes at the source: =TRIM(B5) in a helper Z5:Z94, then paste its values over B5:B94.
  2. Parse the channel out of every memo into W5:W94 with MID and FIND.
  3. Turn the text amounts into numbers: =VALUE(E5) in Z5:Z94, paste its values over E5:E94, then clear the helper.
  4. Split the codes into the cluster in S5:S94 and the site in T5:T94 with one Text to Columns on the hyphen.
  5. Read the checks on Summary in C80:C83: with the dump clean, all four read zero.
