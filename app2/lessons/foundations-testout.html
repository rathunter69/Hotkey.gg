Lesson 39: Test out of Foundations

Foundations · Project and assessment · hard · Free

undefined

What you will do

  1. The Report shows its gridlines: turn them off, as a page someone reads should be.
  2. Every week label on the Report still reads w/c 08 Sep: replace all of them with w/c 15 Sep in one pass.
  3. The panes are unfrozen, so the headers scroll away: freeze them at B5 so rows 1–4 and column A stay in view.
  4. The second site’s revenue in D6 is a typed number where its link belongs: make it =Raw!J9 again.
  5. The average prices in G5:G11 lost their format: show them as currency with two decimals.
  6. Margin % is hidden between G and I: select G:I and unhide it.
  7. The gross-profit total in F11 was retyped as a number: make it =SUM(F5:F10) again.
  8. On Inputs the wholesale price in B4 is an input shown black: color it blue.
  9. On Costs the typed figures in B4:D8 read black: select the constants with Go To Special and color them blue.
  10. On Raw the site name in B14 has a doubled last letter: open it and remove the extra letter.
  11. Does it tie? Watch the first site’s Monday on Raw change to 3,000 and the Total in C11 answer, with the checks below still reading zero.
