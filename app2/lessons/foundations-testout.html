Lesson 58: Test out of Foundations

Foundations · Project and assessment · hard · Free

Already know this chapter? Prove it: eight tasks across sheets, moving, selecting, editing, structure, the Ribbon and formulas, in four minutes. Pass and every Foundations lesson is marked complete; fall short and nothing is lost — the chapter is there to learn from.

What you will do

  1. Rename the sheet tab from Sheet1 to Report.
  2. Select the report block A1:C7 in one jump with Go To.
  3. Select the whole of column C, the Units column, with one chord.
  4. Replace every Draft in the Status column with Final in one pass.
  5. Freeze the top row so the title stays put.
  6. Type Total into A8, then select B3:C8 and AutoSum both columns at once.
  7. Rule the table with All Borders on A2:C8.
  8. In B10, divide total Sales by total Units with a formula for the average price.
