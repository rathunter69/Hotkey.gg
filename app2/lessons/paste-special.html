Lesson 34: Paste Special

Foundations · Copy, paste and fill · hard · Free

A plain paste brings everything; Paste Special brings exactly one aspect. In this lesson you freeze formulas into values, copy just a format, turn a row into a column with Transpose, and multiply a whole range by a copied factor. Values-only paste is the single most used trick in modelling.

What you will do

  1. Paste Special (Ctrl+Alt+V) pastes one aspect: values V, formats T, transpose E, or an operation. Copy the Revenue formulas D3:D7 and paste only their values onto F3.
  2. Copy the styled Sales header B2 and paste only its format onto the Snapshot label in F2.
  3. Copy the headers A2:D2 and transpose them down onto A16.
  4. Copy the 1.1 uplift in H2 and multiply it into the Sales figures B3:B7 in one paste.
