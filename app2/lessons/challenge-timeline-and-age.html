Lesson 62: Challenge: a fiscal-quarter timeline and an age table

Formulas and functions · Dates · hard · Paid

undefined

What you will do

  1. On Sites, age each site in years with YEARFRAC from its opening date in E5:E10 to the as-of date in I5, in K5:K10.
  2. Give each site its vintage, the year it opened, in P5:P10.
  3. On Transactions, write the month key as text, like 2026-09, in G5:G94 and the quarter number in I5:I94.
  4. The buyer’s fiscal year ends June 30: its year in L5:L94 and its half, H1 or H2, in M5:M94.
  5. Flag every Saturday and Sunday wash 1, and every weekday wash 0, in Q5:Q94.
  6. On Sites, count each site’s trading days in R5:R10: seven days a week from opening to the as-of date, less the holidays in C13:C14.
