Lesson 44: Challenge: a raw P&L to standard in three minutes

Formatting and presentation · Number formats · medium · Paid

undefined

What you will do

  1. The figure block B5:D18 is General with the export’s stray cents: give it comma style with no decimals.
  2. Energy cost B9:D9 and the four operating cost lines B12:D15 are typed positive: park a -1 in B30 and multiply them by it with Paste Special.
  3. Gross profit B10:D10 and EBITDA B17:D17 still subtract: rewrite each as a sum, =B8+B9 and =B10+B16, filled right with Ctrl+R.
  4. Rows 5, 8, 10, 16 and 17 carry the $: give each line Accounting Number Format with Alt H A N.
  5. Gross margin % B11:D11 and EBITDA margin % B18:D18 are fractions: show each as a percentage to one decimal.
  6. The site count B20:D20 arrived dressed as dollars: return it to General, then give Revenue per site B21:D21 currency with no decimals.
  7. State the convention in A2, USD unless stated; costs shown as negatives, and clear the -1 out of B30.
