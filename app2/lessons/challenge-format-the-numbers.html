Lesson 45: Challenge: a raw P&L’s numbers to standard

Formatting and presentation · Number formats · medium · Paid

A fresh export from another cluster has the same faults as the company’s. Bring its formats, signs, margins and dates to standard in three minutes.

What you will do

  1. Give the dollar lines C7:E10, C13:E20 and C22:E24 the desk number format from Ctrl+1, and F4.
  2. Park a -1 in G19, copy it, and Paste Special Multiply the costs C13:E19 and head office C23:E23 by it.
  3. Make site contribution C22 =C10+C20 and EBITDA C24 =C22+C23, each filled right, then clear G19.
  4. State the convention in A2: USD thousands unless stated; costs shown as negatives.
  5. Show the margins block C27:E30 as percentages to one decimal, in italic.
  6. Put the $ on C7:E7, C10:E10 and C24:E24 as currency with no decimals.
  7. Type 12/31/2024, 12/31/2025 and 12/31/2026 into C4:E4 and show them as dates with Ctrl+1, Date, mmm-yy.
