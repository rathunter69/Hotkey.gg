Lesson 41: Sign convention: costs negative

Formatting and presentation · Number formats · medium · Paid

undefined

What you will do

  1. Park a -1 well below the page: Go To B30, type -1 and press Enter, step back up onto it and copy it with Ctrl+C.
  2. Paste Special’s Operation multiplies the copied value into every selected cell: Ctrl+Alt+V, V for values, M for Multiply, Enter — the figures flip, nothing is retyped. Energy cost B9:P9 is a cost: jump to B9, select the line with Ctrl+Shift+→, and multiply it by the copied -1 with Paste Special.
  3. The four operating cost lines B12:P15 are costs too: select the block from B12 and multiply it by the same -1 in one paste.
  4. Gross profit still subtracts: in B10 write it as the sum =B8+B9, then fill the line right to P10 with Ctrl+R.
  5. EBITDA in B17 becomes the sum =B10+B16 the same way, filled right to P17 with Ctrl+R.
  6. State the convention once, in the units line A2: USD unless stated; costs shown as negatives.
  7. The -1 in B30 has done its job: Go To B30 and clear it with Delete, so nothing stray sits below the page.
  8. Does it tie? Watch FY24A’s energy cost in B9 change to (5,000,000) and Gross profit in B10 fall with it, a cost added, not subtracted.
