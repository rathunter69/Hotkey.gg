Lesson 217: Challenge: a paper LBO

Valuation · LBO · hard · Paid

Fresh inputs, one sheet, one senior tranche. Sources and uses, the sweep over five years, exit equity, MOIC and IRR, the bridge, and the flag against 20%.

What you will do

  1. Sources and uses balancing: total uses in C39, the sponsor’s equity in C43 and total sources in C44.
  2. Five years of the senior loan with its sweep, closing FY31 at H75, and net debt at FY31 in H97.
  3. Equity at the exit in C104: the exit multiple on FY31 EBITDA, less net debt.
  4. MOIC in C106 and IRR on the equity cash flows in C108.
  5. The bridge in C122:C127: the gain, growth, multiple, paydown and fees, and their total.
  6. The flag in C119: Clears or Short against the 20% hurdle.
