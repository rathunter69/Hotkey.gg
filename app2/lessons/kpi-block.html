Lesson 123: The KPI block: washes per hour, member share, utilization

Data and Lookups · Summaries from raw rows · hard · Paid

Utilization is washes done against washes the tunnel could do, capacity in cars an hour times hours open, and an express wash runs well under half on an average day and fills on a Saturday, so the peak matters as much as the mean. Member share is member washes over all washes; the higher it is, the steadier the revenue. Both are ratios built from the export and the site list, with a lookup bringing hours in. Build the KPI block by site. The key is INDEX.

What you will do

  1. Hours open by site in E5:E10 from Lists: =INDEX(Lists!$G$5:$G$10,MATCH($B5,Lists!$B$5:$B$10,0)) with Ctrl+Enter.
  2. Daily capacity in F5:F10 =D5*E5, and the days each site reported in G5:G10 with COUNTIFS on the site and hours ">0".
  3. A ratio is a figure over the base it is measured against. Utilization is washes over what the tunnel could have done in the days it reported, capacity a day times days, so H5/(F5*G5); percent to one decimal, in italics, like every ratio on the page. Washes by site in H5:H10 with SUMIFS on the export, then utilization in I5:I10 =H5/(F5*G5).
  4. The peak day in J5:J10 with MAXIFS on the site, and its utilization in K5:K10 =J5/F5.
  5. Member washes in L5:L10, a SUMIFS on column D of the export, and member share in M5:M10 =L5/H5.
  6. The total row: SUM for F11, H11 and L11, MAX for J11, and the ratios from totals, I11 =H11/SUMPRODUCT(F5:F10,G5:G10) and M11 =L11/H11.
  7. On Q&A, set questions 2 and 3 to Answered and point their answers at Summary: F6 =Summary!I5 and F7 =Summary!M11.
  8. Does it tie? Watch Airport’s capacity on Lists go to 160: its utilization in I9 falls and its washes in H9 stay put.
