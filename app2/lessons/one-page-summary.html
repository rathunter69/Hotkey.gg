Lesson 52: The one-page summary linked from the detail

Formatting and presentation · Printing and page layout · medium · Paid

undefined

What you will do

  1. On Print, type the six labels down B5:B10: Revenue, Site contribution, EBITDA, EBITDA margin, Sites (year end), Washes (thousands).
  2. A summary holds no typed figure: every number is a link to the detail behind it, so the two can never disagree. With C5:E5 selected, one pointed link and Ctrl+Enter writes all three, each reading its own year. Select Print!C5:E5, type =, point at P&L!C10 across sheets and press Ctrl+Enter for a row of links to total revenue.
  3. Link rows 6 to 10 the same way, to P&L rows 22, 24, 29, 33 and 34: site contribution, EBITDA, its margin, sites and washes.
  4. Under the lines, put Source: the P&L sheet in B11 in italic, Checks in B13 in bold and Last year EBITDA less the P&L in B14, indented.
  5. In C14, point =E7-'P&L'!E24 so the check reads zero, and give it the red check code 0_);[Red](0);-_).
  6. Bring the P&L's number formats onto C5:E10 with Paste Formats, Ctrl+Alt+V then T: lines from C7:E8, margins from C27:E27, memo from C33:E34.
  7. Every figure on Print is a link to another sheet: turn C5:E10 green with Alt, H, F, C.
  8. Draw the A/E divider on Print: a right border down D4:D10 with Alt, H, B, R, and the gray shade on E4 with Alt, H, H.
  9. Lay Print out like the P&L: A at width 2, B fitted over B4:B10, C:E at 12, gridlines off and the panes frozen at C5.
  10. Does it tie? Watch P&L!E7 change to 27000, and Print's revenue in E5 and EBITDA in E7 answer while the check in C14 stays at zero.
