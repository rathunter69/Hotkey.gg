Lesson 30: Go To: cells, ranges and other sheets

Foundations · Moving · easy · Free

When you know the address, do not travel — name it. In this lesson Go To (Ctrl+G) takes you to a cell, selects a whole range, and lands on another sheet of the workbook in one jump. Analysts navigate big models this way all day.

What you will do

  1. Go To (Ctrl+G or F5) jumps to any reference you type. Go straight to B40, the note at the bottom, with Ctrl+G.
  2. Go To A1:C7 to select the whole report block in one jump.
  3. A reference on another sheet names the sheet first: Costs!B3. Go To Costs!B3 — Monday’s costs on the Costs sheet.
