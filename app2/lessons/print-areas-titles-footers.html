Lesson 51: Print areas, titles, fit to width, headers and footers

Formatting and presentation · Printing and page layout · medium · Paid

undefined

What you will do

  1. The P&L and Monthly both run wider than they are tall: turn the pack landscape with Alt, P, O, L.
  2. Open Page Setup with Alt, P, S, P and set Fit to 1 page wide by 2 tall, so Monthly runs down the page and not off it.
  3. Repeat the title, units line, timeline and flags on every printed page: Print Titles with Alt, P, I, rows 1:5.
  4. A pack prints many pages, so the footer numbers them: &[Page] is this page and &[Pages] the count, and Page &[Page] of &[Pages] reads Page 2 of 3. Alt+L, Alt+C and Alt+R move between the three sections of Custom Footer. In Page Setup's Custom Footer, Alt+U, put &[File] left, Page &[Page] of &[Pages] in the center and &[Date] right.
  5. Center on page puts the same white space either side of the figures, so a page narrower than the paper sits in the middle. Excel's Normal margins keep the header margin, 0.3 inches, under the top margin, 0.75, so the header never prints over the title row. On Page Setup's Margins tab, tick Center on page Horizontally with Alt+Z and keep the margins as they are.
  6. Does it tie? Watch C7 change to 18500 and total revenue in C10 answer: the print set-up changed nothing on the sheet.
