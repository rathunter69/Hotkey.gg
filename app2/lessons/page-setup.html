Lesson 5: Page setup and best practices

Foundations · How Excel works · medium · Free

Two habits separate a sheet that prints and reads well from one that does not: page setup and the colour convention. In this lesson you set the Weekly Sales Report to Landscape and Fit to 1 page in Page Setup, then colour its hardcoded inputs blue and leave the formulas black. Keep one hardcode per cell, in blue, and anyone can see what to change without breaking a formula.

What you will do

  1. Alt, P, S, P opens the Page Setup dialog box from the Page Layout tab, where L picks Landscape, T Portrait, and Enter presses OK. Set the page orientation to Landscape.
  2. In the same dialog box F chooses Fit to 1 page wide by 1 tall, so a wide sheet prints on one page instead of spilling onto a second. Open Page Setup again and choose Fit to 1 page wide by 1 tall.
  3. Models colour hardcoded inputs blue and formulas black so a reader knows what to change: Alt, H, F, C opens Font Color, → moves along the swatches, Enter applies. Select the three inputs B3:B5 and make them blue.
