Lesson 56: TEXT for labels and headers

Formatting and presentation · Dates and text for presentation · medium · Paid

undefined

What you will do

  1. TEXT(value, "code") writes a value through a format code and returns text: the codes are the ones you typed in the Custom box, so mmmm is the month in full and yyyy the year. In Monthly!C5, under the first month end, enter =TEXT(C4,"mmmm yyyy") and read January 2026.
  2. Ctrl+Shift+← from N5 stops at the first filled cell, C5, so the selection runs C5:N5 and Ctrl+R fills it from the left. Fill C5 right to N5 with Ctrl+R, one month name under each date.
  3. With C5:N5 still selected, set the month names in italic with Ctrl+I and right-align them under the dates.
  4. The & joins text to text: "FY", the two-digit year from TEXT, and the A or E flag under the date make FY24A. A sheet name with an & in it takes single quotes. In Print!C4 enter ="FY"&TEXT('P&L'!C4,"yy")&'P&L'!C5 and read FY24A.
  5. Fill C4 right to E4 with Ctrl+R to read FY24A, FY25A, FY26E.
  6. Make C4:E4 bold and right-aligned, and color them green: they read another sheet.
  7. In Inputs!B19 build the headline Revenue of 50,000k in FY26E with & and TEXT('P&L'!E10,"#,##0"), and color it green.
  8. Does it tie? Watch the FY26 flag in P&L!E5 change to A, and the header in Print!E4 read FY26A.
