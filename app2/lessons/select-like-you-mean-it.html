Lesson 8: Select like you mean it

Foundations · Move and select · easy · Free

undefined

What you will do

  1. Ctrl+PgDn from Report. Every selection in this lesson starts from a cell you’ve landed on by keyboard. Move to Raw, where the selecting is.
  2. Hold Ctrl and Shift together and press ↓: the selection runs from the active cell to the edge of the data. The Name Box reads E1:E60. Sixty cells in one press. Select Revenue from its header to the last figure: land on E1, then one Ctrl+Shift+↓.
  3. Same status bar as 1.1.2, bigger selection: sixty cells summed with no formula written. Do this on every column you’re about to use. It’s how you catch a text figure or a missing day before it costs you. With E1:E60 still selected, read the status bar at the bottom of the window (sum, average, count), then come back to A1 with Ctrl+Home.
  4. Shift+Space selects the entire row of the active cell, edge to edge. Any arrow key on its own collapses a selection back to one cell. Select the whole header row with Shift+Space.
  5. Ctrl+Space selects the entire column. Row and column selections are how you insert, delete, hide and resize, all of module 1.4. On a Mac, ⌃Space may be taken by the system; ⌘⇧↑ then ⌘⇧↓ from the top of a column does the job. Now the whole of column E with Ctrl+Space.
  6. Ctrl+A selects the current region: the block of data around the active cell, bounded by blank rows and columns. From inside the feed, that’s A1:F61. Select the whole feed in one press: Ctrl+A.
  7. Ctrl+A again widens to every cell on the sheet. Useful for a format you want everywhere, dangerous for anything else. Press Ctrl+A a second time: the entire sheet.
  8. Ctrl+Shift+End selects from the active cell to the last used cell on the sheet, gaps included. Ctrl+A stops at the first blank row; Ctrl+Shift+End doesn’t. Go home to A1 with Ctrl+Home, then take everything in use (feed, notes and the totals block) with Ctrl+Shift+End.
