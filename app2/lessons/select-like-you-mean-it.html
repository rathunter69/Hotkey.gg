Lesson 7: Select like you mean it

Foundations · Move and select · easy · Free

undefined

What you will do

  1. Move to Raw, where the selecting is.
  2. Select Revenue from its header to the last figure: E1, then one Ctrl+Shift+↓.
  3. Shift+Space selects the active row; Ctrl+Space selects the active column. Collapse to A1, then select the whole header row with Shift+Space.
  4. Now the whole of column E with Ctrl+Space.
  5. Ctrl+A selects the current region — the block of data around the active cell. Select the whole feed in one press: Ctrl+A.
  6. Ctrl+A pressed again widens to the entire sheet. Press Ctrl+A a second time: the entire sheet.
  7. Collapse to A1 with Ctrl+Home, then take everything used — feed, notes and the totals block — with Ctrl+Shift+End.
