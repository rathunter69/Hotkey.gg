Lesson 163: Terminal value: perpetuity and exit multiple

Finance and Accounting · DCF · hard · Paid

The forecast stops at FY31 and the business doesn’t, so the terminal value stands for everything after. The perpetuity method grows a normalized last year’s cash flow at a steady rate forever; the exit multiple method sells the business in FY31 at a multiple of EBITDA, the way a sponsor will. Build both, then read the implied multiple of one and the implied growth of the other, so each checks the other. The key is =.

What you will do

  1. FY31 is still a rollout year, so its raw cash flow would charge six new sites’ capex forever. Column K is a normalized FY31 beside the forecast; it starts from FY31’s EBITDA and depreciation. Carry FY31 into the normalized column: K8 =J8, and K9 =J9.
  2. The rest of the column runs the same formulas as the forecast, so Ctrl+R fills them across from FY31. Fill J10:K13 and J16:K17 across with Ctrl+R.
  3. In a steady year capex only replaces what wears out, so it is set to depreciation; working capital moves by its steady amount from Inputs. Set K14 to =-K13 and K15 to =Inputs!$C$98.
  4. The perpetuity grows the normalized cash flow a year and divides by WACC less growth. Growth is 3%, no faster than the economy, and it has to sit below WACC or the formula breaks. The perpetuity value in C27: =K16*(1+Inputs!$C$95)/(WACC-Inputs!$C$95).
  5. The exit multiple sells the business at the end of FY31 for 11.0 times that year’s EBITDA, sourced to the listed operators and the precedent deals. The exit value in C28: =J8*Inputs!$C$94.
  6. Each method implies the other’s input: the perpetuity value over FY31 EBITDA is a multiple, and the perpetuity formula solved for growth turns the exit value into a growth rate. Implied multiple in C29: =C27/J8, and implied growth in C30: =(C28*WACC-K16)/(C28+K16).
  7. Feed the implied multiple back into the exit method, solve for growth, and the growth input must come back. If it doesn’t, the two values aren’t built on the same cash flow. The round trip in C31: =((C29*J8)*WACC-K16)/((C29*J8)+K16), then on Checks C26: =ROUND(DCF!C31-Inputs!$C$95,6).
  8. The switch: C32 =Inputs!$C$96, and C33 =CHOOSE(C32,C27,C28).
  9. Does it tie? Watch the exit multiple on Inputs go from 11.0x to 13.0x: the implied growth in C30 climbs.
