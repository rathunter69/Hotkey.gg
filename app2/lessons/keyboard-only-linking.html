Lesson 197: Link the cash flow by keyboard alone

Finance and Accounting · Model speed · medium · Paid

The cash flow statement has its labels, formats and subtotals, and not one link: net income, depreciation, working capital, capex, the debt lines and the revolver are all empty. Link it without touching the mouse: select a row, type the reference once and press Ctrl+Enter, so every year links to its own column. Then the totals the same way, and green on every link through Font Color and F4. The key is Ctrl+Enter.

What you will do

  1. A link row is one entry: select the row from FY24 to FY31, type the reference for FY24 and press Ctrl+Enter, and each column reads its own year because the reference is relative. Typing the reference is faster than pointing at it on another sheet, and Ctrl+G gets you to the row. Link net income, =IS!C31 into CF C6:J6, and depreciation, =Schedules!C64 into C7:J7, each with Ctrl+Enter.
  2. The three working capital changes sit in Schedules rows 53 to 55: select C8:J10 and enter =Schedules!C53 with Ctrl+Enter.
  3. Capex is cash going out: enter =-Schedules!C63 into C14:J14 with Ctrl+Enter.
  4. Link the term loan, =Schedules!C80 into C18:J19, and the delayed draw, =Schedules!C90 into C20:J21, each with Ctrl+Enter.
  5. The revolver nets its draw and repayment: enter =Schedules!C101+Schedules!C102 into C24:J24 with Ctrl+Enter.
  6. Net change in cash is =C11+C15+C25 in C28:J28, and closing cash =C29+C28 in C30:J30, each with Ctrl+Enter.
  7. Color the links green: C6:J10 through Font Color, then F4 on C14:J14, C18:J21 and C24:J24.
  8. Does it tie? Watch FY31 washes per day in Inputs J22 go from 250 to 300: closing cash climbs and the cash check, Checks J7, holds at 0.
