Lesson 41: Relative and absolute references

Foundations · Basic formulas · medium · Free

Fill a formula down and its references walk with it — usually what you want, fatal for the one cell that must not move. In this lesson $ anchors the commission rate in E1 so every filled row still reads it, and F4 types the anchors for you. This distinction is the number one beginner formula bug.

What you will do

  1. A relative reference (B3) shifts when copied; $ anchors it: $B$3 never moves. In C3, enter =B3*$E$1: Monday’s commission at the anchored rate.
  2. Select C3:C7 and fill down — every row shifts its day but keeps the one rate.
  3. F4 cycles the anchors on the reference at the insertion point: E1, $E$1, E$1, $E1. In D3, type =B3*E1, press F4 to anchor the rate, then commit.
