Lesson 27: Undo, redo and Escape

Foundations · Entering and editing · easy · Free

Mistakes are cheap when you can take them back. In this lesson you overwrite a figure and undo it, bring it back with redo, and use Escape to abandon an entry before it ever lands. Fearless undo is what lets you work fast.

What you will do

  1. Ctrl+Z undoes the last change; Ctrl+Y redoes it. Type 0 over Tuesday’s 950 in B4, press Enter, then undo it with Ctrl+Z.
  2. Bring the change back with Ctrl+Y.
  3. Undo once more so Tuesday reads 950 again.
  4. Start typing 999 over Friday’s figure in B7, then discard it with Esc before it lands.
