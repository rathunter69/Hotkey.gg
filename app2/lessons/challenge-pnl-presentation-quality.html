What you will do

  1. Make the title in A1 bold and one size up, and center it across A1:E1.
  2. Type the section headers Revenue in B6, Site costs in B12 and Memo in B32, bold all four headers and the four answer lines.
  3. Indent the sub-lines B7:B9 and B13:B19 one level with Alt, H, 6.
  4. Take the grid off C7:E24, give the four totals a top border and EBITDA a double bottom.
  5. Put the divider down D4:D35 with Alt, H, B, R and shade the estimate header E4:E5 gray.
  6. Retype the revenue labels B7:B10 in sentence case, and type the source line in B36 in italic.
  7. Make column A 2 wide, the years C:E 12, AutoFit B over B4:B35, and turn the gridlines off.
