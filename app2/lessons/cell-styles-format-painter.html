Lesson 56: One page’s formats carried to the next

Formatting and presentation · The page a buyer reads · medium · Paid

undefined

What you will do

  1. Paste Special Formats (Ctrl+Alt+V, T) pastes how the cells look and nothing they hold. A one-column source pasted over a wider block repeats across every column, so one P&L column dresses all thirteen. Copy P&L!C6:C30 and paste its formats over Monthly!C6:O30 in one paste with Ctrl+Alt+V, T.
  2. The label column takes its own formats: copy P&L!B6:B30 and paste formats onto Monthly!B6.
  3. The P&L’s column C is blue because it is typed; Monthly’s full year is formulas, so it goes back to black. On Ctrl+1’s Font tab, Alt+C reaches the color and ← steps back to Automatic. The full year O7:O24 is formulas, not typed: set its font color back to Automatic from Ctrl+1’s Font tab.
  4. Copy the P&L’s title cell A1, paste its formats onto Monthly!A1, then center it across A1:O1.
  5. Cell Styles (Alt, H, J) name a look so a page can use it by name. New Cell Style (N) saves the active cell’s look, and its ticks choose the parts it carries: untick Number (Alt+N) and each figure keeps its own code. Total is Excel’s own style, so this one takes its own name. On P&L!B10, open New Cell Style with Alt, H, J, N, name it Total line, untick Number with Alt+N and press Enter.
  6. Apply Total line to Monthly’s subtotals B10:O10, B20:O20 and B22:O22: Alt, H, J, ← once to it under Custom, Enter.
  7. Give Monthly the P&L’s widths: column A 2, and the months and full year C:O 12.
  8. Fit Monthly’s label column to its labels: select B4:B30 and AutoFit with Alt, H, O, I.
  9. Freeze Monthly’s panes at C5 with Alt, W, F, F and turn its gridlines off with Alt, W, V, G.
  10. Does it tie? Watch Monthly!C7 change to 2000, and Monthly!C24 answer in the borrowed format.
