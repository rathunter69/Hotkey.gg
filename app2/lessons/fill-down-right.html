Lesson 28: Fill down, fill right, Ctrl+Enter

Foundations · Entering and editing · medium · Free

Once one cell is right, the rest of its row or column is a fill, not a retype. In this lesson Ctrl+D fills the owner label down, Ctrl+R carries the Total formula across the regions, and Ctrl+Enter drops one entry into a whole selection. Filling is how a model row gets built in seconds.

What you will do

  1. Ctrl+D fills the selection from its top row; Ctrl+R fills it from its left column. Select A3:A7 and fill Sales team down with Ctrl+D.
  2. Select B9:D9 and carry the Total formula across with Ctrl+R.
  3. Ctrl+Enter commits an entry into every selected cell at once. Select E3:E7, type 0 and land it in all five cells with one Ctrl+Enter.
