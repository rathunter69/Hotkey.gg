Lesson 51: Title, units, timeline, sections, answer

Formatting and presentation · The page a buyer reads · medium · Paid

A financial page reads top to bottom in one order: what it is, what it’s in, when, the lines, the answer. The title in A1 says which company and which statement; the units line under it says the currency and the sign convention; the timeline is row 4; the sections are Revenue, Site costs, Site contribution, Head office, EBITDA. And EBITDA is the answer, so the page is built to land on it. Put the anatomy in place. The key is Ctrl+B.

What you will do

  1. The title says which company and which statement, and it is the one line on the page a size up. Increase Font Size is Alt, H, F, G, one step up the size list. Type "Clearcoat Express - Historical Financials" in A1, bold it with Ctrl+B and take it one size up with Alt, H, F, G.
  2. Center the title across A1:E1 with Ctrl+1, Alignment, Center Across Selection, so it sits over the figures without a merge.
  3. Sections group the lines a reader adds in their head: what came in, what the sites cost, and the blocks under the answer. The units line in A2 is already there from module 2.1, italic, saying the currency and that costs show as negatives. Type Revenue in B6, Site costs in B12 and Memo in B32, and bold all four section headers, Margins and growth in B26 too.
  4. Bold is for the lines the page adds down to, and nothing else: total revenue, total site costs, site contribution and EBITDA. F4 repeats the last action, so one Ctrl+B does all four. Bold the answer lines B24:E24, B22:E22, B20:E20 and B10:E10, with Ctrl+B on the first and F4 on the rest.
  5. An indent says a line belongs to the total under it. Increase Indent is Alt, H, 6 and Decrease Indent is Alt, H, 5, one level at a time. Indent the sub-lines B7:B9 once with Alt, H, 6, then B13:B19 with F4.
  6. The checks block takes the same anatomy: bold its header B38 and indent B39:B40 once.
  7. Typed figures are blue and formulas black, on the page as in the model, so a reader knows which numbers came in from the ledger. Go To Special Constants picks the typed cells out of a block and leaves the formulas. Select C7:E34, keep only the typed figures with Go To Special Constants (Alt, H, F, D, N), and color them blue.
  8. Monthly takes the same rule: select its months C7:N23 and color only the typed figures blue with Go To Special Constants again.
  9. The account codes belong to the ledger, not to the page. Clear All (Alt, H, E, A) takes the contents and the formats, so nothing of them is left behind. Back on the P&L, select the account codes and their header in A4:A23 and clear them with Alt, H, E, A.
  10. Does it tie? Watch C7 change to 18500, and EBITDA in C24 answer under the title.
