Lesson 179: The balance sheet, and cash as the plug that isn’t a plug

Finance and Accounting · Linking the statements · medium · Paid

The balance sheet links last: receivables, payables and deferred revenue from working capital, PP&E from its roll, debt from its schedule, equity as opening plus net income less distributions, and cash from the cash flow’s closing line. Cash looks like a plug but isn’t, because it was built from every other line. Link it, and the check on Checks goes live. The key is =.

What you will do

  1. Cash looks like the plug, the number that makes the sheet balance, but it isn’t: it was built from every other line on the cash flow. So if the sheet balances, every link is right, and if it doesn’t, one of them is wrong. Assets in C6:J10: cash from the cash flow’s closing line, receivables, land at cost, PP&E, then total assets.
  2. Liabilities in C13:J18: payables, deferred revenue, the three debt closings, the revolver included, and the total.
  3. Equity in C21:J25: opening, net income, distributions, closing, then total liabilities and equity.
  4. The balance check in C28:J28, then on Checks the debt and PP&E ties in C8:J9; every one reads zero.
  5. Go to the checks flag in C7 of Cover and read it: OK, every check at zero.
  6. Does it tie? Watch capex per site go to $6.0m: cash goes below zero, and the check still reads zero in every year.
