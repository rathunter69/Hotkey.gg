Lesson 30: One formula per row, filled right

Foundations · Formulas · medium · Free

undefined

What you will do

  1. Domain’s Monday in B17 is empty: link it to Domain’s Monday I32 in Raw’s by-day block, pointing across sheets.
  2. Write the row once: with Monday’s link at the left of B17:G17, fill it right and all six days take the same pattern.
  3. Ctrl+` shows every formula’s text in place of its value, so a typed number stands out from the links; press it again to return. One of the associate’s cells is a number, not a link: show every formula in the daily table and find the one that stands out, E19.
  4. Riverside’s Thursday E19 was retyped: refill B19:G19 from its own Monday formula with F2 then Ctrl+Enter, never by retyping.
  5. Bring the values back to the daily table: every cell in B17:G21 now reads as a live link.
  6. A link to another sheet is shown green, the format the team uses: select B17:G17 and color Domain’s row with Alt H F C.
  7. Does it tie? Watch Domain’s Monday kWh on Raw’s C8 change to 3,000 and the Report’s B17 answer through the by-day block.
