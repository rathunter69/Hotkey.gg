Lesson 163: Inputs, calculations, outputs: architecture and sheet order

Finance and Accounting · Model setup and efficiencies · medium · Paid

A model reads left to right the way it calculates: Cover, Inputs, the three statements, the schedules that feed them, Checks, then the DCF. Inputs are blue and live on one sheet, calculations never hold a typed number, and outputs are the pages someone reads. The Cover carries the title, the case switch, the checks flag and a map of the sheets with links (2.4.4). Put the eight sheets in order with the Cover first, then finish the Cover. The key is Alt H O M.

What you will do

  1. A model runs in the order it calculates: inputs feed the schedules, the schedules feed the statements, the statements feed the DCF. The tabs read the same way, so a reader follows the money left to right. Move Cover to the front: Ctrl+PgDn to it, then Alt, H, O, M and ↑ to the top of the list.
  2. Best practice: every typed number lives on Inputs, in blue, and nowhere else. A typed number on any other sheet is a fault the audit in 5.5 will find. Inputs goes second, straight after the Cover.
  3. Then the three statements in the order a DCF reads them: IS, CF, BS.
  4. The schedules sit behind the statements they feed, Checks behind everything it tests, and the DCF last because it reads the lot. Schedules, Checks and DCF close the row, so the tabs read Cover to DCF.
  5. The Cover takes its title from Inputs, where the company name is typed once (2.6.3), so a renamed file renames every page. On the Cover, the title in A1 from Inputs: =Inputs!$C$104&": operating model".
  6. The switch in C5 is the word a buyer types; MATCH turns it into the number the drivers block reads, named Case (4.5.1). The flag reads Checks, so the Cover says at a glance whether the model ties. The case number in C6, =MATCH(C5,C23:C25,0), and the checks flag in C7, =Checks!C45.
  7. A link per sheet makes the Cover the contents page (2.4.4). The rest of the map is in place; these two show the pattern. The map: =HYPERLINK("#Inputs!A1","Inputs") in C11, and the same for IS in C12.
  8. Does it tie? Watch the tabs go by from the Cover: inputs, statements, schedules, checks, then the DCF.
