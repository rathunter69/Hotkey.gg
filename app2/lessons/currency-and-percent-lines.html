Lesson 43: Currency and percent lines

Formatting and presentation · Number formats · medium · Paid

undefined

What you will do

  1. Type Margins and growth in B26, then Gross margin, Site contribution margin, EBITDA margin and Revenue growth down B27:B30.
  2. A margin is a line as a share of revenue, and gross margin adds the negative chemicals line to revenue: what is left of each dollar after the wash. Type =(C10+C13)/C10 in C27 for gross margin, then select C27:E27 and fill it right with Ctrl+R.
  3. Type =C22/C10 in C28 and =C24/C10 in C29, then select C28:E29 and fill both right with Ctrl+R.
  4. Growth starts in the second year: type =D10/C10-1 in D30, fill it right to E30, and leave C30 empty.
  5. CAGR, compound annual growth, is the one growth figure a buyer quotes, and COLUMNS(C10:E10)-1 counts its periods, three columns less one, so no 2 gets typed. Label F29 CAGR, then put the two-year CAGR in F30: =(E10/C10)^(1/(COLUMNS(C10:E10)-1))-1.
  6. Select C27:F30 and make it percentages to one decimal with Ctrl+Shift+5 and Alt H 0, then italic with Ctrl+I.
  7. Put the $ on C7:E7, C10:E10 and C24:E24 as currency with no decimals: Ctrl+1, Currency, 0 decimal places, and F4 for the next two.
  8. Revenue per wash in C35:E35 is dollars and cents: give it currency with two decimals, Ctrl+Shift+4.
  9. Does it tie? Watch membership revenue in E8 change to 24000, and EBITDA margin E29 and growth E30 move with it.
