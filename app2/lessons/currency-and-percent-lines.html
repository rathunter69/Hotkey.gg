Lesson 42: Currency and percent lines

Formatting and presentation · Number formats · medium · Paid

undefined

What you will do

  1. Accounting Number Format, Alt H A N, puts the $ at the cell’s left edge, negatives in parentheses and zero as a dash: the dress a $ row wears. Public charging B5:P5 is the block’s first row: select it and give it Accounting Number Format with Alt H A N.
  2. Total revenue B8:P8 is a total: three rows down, select the line and give it the same Accounting format.
  3. Gross profit B10:P10 is a total too: two rows down, the same selection and the same format.
  4. Gross margin % B11:P11 is a fraction: one row down, select it, Ctrl+Shift+5 for percent, then Alt H 0 for one decimal.
  5. Total operating costs and EBITDA, B16:P17, are totals too: jump to the foot, up two, select both lines and apply Alt H A N.
  6. EBITDA margin % B18:P18 reads as a percentage to one decimal, like the gross margin above it.
  7. Does it tie? Watch FY24A’s public charging in B5 change to $ 9,000,000 and the gross margin in B11 answer to one decimal.
