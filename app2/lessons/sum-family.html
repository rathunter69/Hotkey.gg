Lesson 28: SUM, AVERAGE, MIN, MAX and COUNT

Foundations · Basic formulas · medium · Free

Adding five cells with + works; adding fifty does not. In this lesson the SUM family takes a range — B3:B8, Saturday still empty — and answers total, average, lowest, highest and how many days actually have sales. These five functions cover most of the arithmetic in real reports.

What you will do

  1. SUM, AVERAGE, MIN, MAX and COUNT each take a range: =SUM(B3:B7). In B10, total the week with =SUM(B3:B8).
  2. In B11, average the same range — blank Saturday is ignored, not counted as zero.
  3. In B12, find the lowest day with MIN.
  4. In B13, find the highest day with MAX.
  5. In B14, count the days that have a sales figure with COUNT.
