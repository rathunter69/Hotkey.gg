Lesson 171: The cost build: per wash, per site, fixed

Finance and Accounting · Schedules · medium · Paid

Costs come in three kinds and each is built its own way: per wash as a share of revenue, per site as a cost per site × average sites growing with inflation, and fixed as a base growing 3% plus a step per new site. The per-site block is already on Schedules. Build cost of sales, the contribution line, head office and EBITDA around it. The key is =.

What you will do

  1. Each cost is built the way it behaves. Chemicals, water and power move with every wash, so cost of sales is a share of revenue off the drivers block; the per-site block below (labor, rent, utilities, maintenance) is a cost per site × average sites, inflated each year; head office is fixed. Cost of sales in C30:J30: revenue times the live cost-of-wash driver, through the flag.
  2. Read it back in C31:J31: cost of sales as a share of revenue, which equals the driver in every projected year.
  3. Site contribution and the contribution margin in C39:J40: revenue less cost of sales less site costs.
  4. A fixed cost doesn’t scale with sites or washes: it grows with inflation and steps up as the support team grows with the rollout. Last year’s cell times one plus the growth, plus the step times new sites. Head office in C41:J41: last year’s figure grown 3%, plus $50k for every new site, through the flag.
  5. EBITDA and its margin in C42:J43; FY26 EBITDA reads $16,600k, the Chapter 2 P&L’s.
  6. Does it tie? Watch rent per site go to $190k: site costs and EBITDA answer in every projected year and no actual one.
