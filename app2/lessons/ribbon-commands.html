Lesson 6: The Ribbon: tabs and commands

Foundations · The Ribbon and dialogs · medium · Free

The Ribbon holds every command in Excel, and Alt lets you reach any of them from the keyboard. In this lesson you format the Weekly Sales Report with KeyTips, pressing the keys one after another and backing out with Esc: bold, a border and centered headers. Ribbon chords are how fast users format without touching the mouse.

What you will do

  1. Alt shows a KeyTip on every tab, H opens Home, and the next KeyTip runs a command: Alt, H, 1 is Bold. Make the title Weekly Sales Report bold.
  2. Select the headers Day, Sales and Units (A2:C2) and make them bold.
  3. Some commands open a menu of choices: Alt, H, B opens Borders and O picks Bottom Border. Put a bottom border under the Friday figures B7:C7.
  4. Alignment works the same way: Alt, H, A, C centers the selection. Center the Sales and Units headers (B2:C2).
