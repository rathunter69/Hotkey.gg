**Proposed opening sequence, not yet approved as exact lesson scope:**
- Introduce one relevant worksheet/interface area at a time, then ask for an action; avoid a
  long click-through tour before the first interaction.
- First apply bold to the actual visible title ("Weekly Sales Report"), spotlighting selection
  and the ribbon. Explain tab → command group → command in context.
- Offer short ribbon discovery tasks: open the relevant tab, reveal a menu, reach a dialog,
  and back out safely. Count reaching a specific UI state only where ribbon navigation is
  explicitly the lesson; ordinary worksheet tasks continue to accept legitimate routes.
- Follow with a small visual repair board: a clipped label, a heading to emphasize, and a
  misformatted percentage. Introduce each concept before asking for it; distribute these across
  short lessons if a novice would otherwise need several new concepts at once.
- Finish with a fresh, lightly changed task using less guidance. Keep Continue primary and
  Try solo available according to the approved lesson/result policy.

Use short demonstrations, relevant callouts, visible before/after effects, calm recovery and
brief success feedback; no lives, forced countdown or new currency/ladder. A normal concept
explanation does not itself mark assistance. Revealed solution steps, guided routes and solution
replays do; helped completion still advances learning without XP. Fresh solo attempts follow
their own assistance/input record. Do not introduce a new mastery score.
