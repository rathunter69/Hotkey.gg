## What the code shows

The product has no AI features. There is no chatbot, no AI hint, no AI grading and no call to any AI provider from the site or its server functions (checked 2026-10-02: no AI SDK or API host in app2/, app2/supabase/functions/ or the Content-Security-Policy in app2/_headers). Grading is a deterministic engine that checks the end state of the sheet. Your data is not sent to any AI provider and is not used to train models.

The course content and the software were written with the help of AI tools by the people who build hotkey.gg, and are reviewed by them.
