# Children and minors (DRAFT)

Status: draft for a licensed professional and Wolf. Not published.
Markers: **[LAWYER]** is a point a lawyer must confirm.

## What the product does today

- Every form that makes an account (Account page, the sign-in pop-out, checkout's sign-in) shows: "You must be 13 or older to make an account." beside the button (app2/ui/components/consent.js, built in R9).
- There is no date-of-birth question. The site is for a general and adult audience (finance learners, analysts, MBAs) and is not directed to children.
- Guest mode stores nothing on our servers, so a younger learner can use Chapter 1 with a parent or teacher without giving us any personal information.
- Public profiles are on by default for every account. **[LAWYER]** consider off by default, which also lowers the risk for 13 to 17 year olds (several US states' minors' privacy and design codes, and the UK Children's Code).

## The statement (for the Privacy Policy and Terms)

hotkey.gg is not directed to children under 13, and you must be 13 or older to make an account. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has made an account, write to privacy@hotkey.gg and we will delete it. Anyone under 13 can still learn with Chapter 1 as a guest, which keeps everything in the browser and sends us nothing personal.

If you are under the age of majority where you live, you need a parent's or guardian's permission to buy Full Access.

## For counsel

- **[LAWYER]** COPPA: confirm a statement plus no age gate is adequate for a general-audience site with no actual knowledge, or whether a neutral age screen is advisable.
- **[LAWYER]** GDPR Art. 8: the digital consent age is 13 to 16 depending on the EU state; our basis for accounts is contract, not consent, but confirm whether the minimum age should be 16 for EU and UK users.
- **[LAWYER]** Schools: a class or desk run by a school that adds students under 13 would need the school's authorisation under COPPA; desks are not open yet, so this is for when they are.
