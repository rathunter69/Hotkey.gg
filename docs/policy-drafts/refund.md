## The rule

- **Your first payment:** a full refund if you ask within 14 days of it. No reason needed.
- **Later monthly payments:** not refunded. Cancel any time from the Account page and you are not charged again; you keep access to the end of the month you paid for.
- **Teams:** **[WOLF]** not decided. Suggested: the first month's invoice on the same 14-day terms, and seats removed mid-month are not prorated. **[LAWYER]** Teams terms may belong in an order form instead.

## How to ask

Email hello@hotkey.gg from the email on your account, or reply to your receipt. We refund within **[5]** working days to the original payment method. Banks take a few more days to show it.

## What a refund does

A full refund ends your Full Access and cancels the subscription at once (the site does this automatically when the refund is made). Your progress, bests and board entries stay; Chapter 1 stays free.

## Payments sold through Link

Payments are sold through Link, Stripe's merchant of record service. If you contact Link about a payment and we do not answer Link within 48 hours, Link may refund you directly, within 60 days of the payment. **[LAWYER]** confirm Link's buyer terms do not give a broader right we must also state.

## Where the law gives you more

If your local law gives you more (for example the EU or UK 14-day right to withdraw from a digital service), that law applies. **[LAWYER]** The EU and UK withdrawal right is usually lost once you start using the digital content with your express consent and acknowledgement; we do not currently collect that acknowledgement at checkout. Decide whether to collect it or simply honour 14 days for everyone (the policy above already does for the first payment).
